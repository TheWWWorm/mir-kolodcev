The novel is written at the intersection of two linguistic styles. Catalian words follow Spanish models, Dangarian words follow German ones, and Ollandian names resemble Slavic names. The author has said that Catalia and Dangar were conceived not as copies of Spain and Prussia, but as embodiments of two different ideas of the state. This dictionary collects words that occur in the text and may be unfamiliar to the reader. Each definition is based on the book; where a concept has its own article, a link is provided. Later plot twists are deliberately excluded from the dictionary.

*Translator’s note: Entries retain the Russian source’s alphabetical grouping and order; the letter headings are transliterated into Latin script.*

## A
- **Arbiter** — the judge of a dueling debate: a person wearing the bracelet of the esfera of Law. The arbiter takes the oath, maintains order, and delivers the verdict; before the debate, the arbiter may be challenged if they know one opponent better than the other. See [[duelnyj-spor]].
- **Ai-Khinar** — the most fearsome of the Ancients, a winged serpent with venomous fangs. His statue, with a human skull in its mouth, stands at the entrance to the Old Port. See [[aj-khinar]], [[staryj-port]].
- **Academy of Arts** — a white tower in Laerta with a circular balcony along which figures of the mirnas move. Here [[alvaro-da-silva|Alvaro]] lectures future writers. See [[akademiya-iskusstv]].
- **Alessina** — the mirna of art and theater, whose symbol is a theatrical mask. Catalians swear by her name, and one of Laerta’s bays is named in her honor. See [[alessina]].
- **Allen** — an esfera’s representative at the [[vysokij-stol|High Table]]. Each esfera has exactly three seats, regardless of its population; the first representative bears the title **prim**, the second **secunda**, and the third **tertia**. See [[alleny]].
- **Alguacil** — a Catalian investigator and legal officer from the esfera of Law, who determines whether the Scroll has been violated and who is guilty. Wears a pointed beard, a mustache, and a white feather of the mirna [[iolanta|Iolanta]] in the hat. See [[algvasily]], [[diego-ingres]].
- **Almagarian bonk** — an expensive, strong, aged drink, sometimes matured for ten years. Plain “bonk” is hard liquor; its aftereffects are treated with coffee with pepper and orange juice. See also **cofenado**.
- **Arshin** — a Dangarian unit of length. A person’s height and the width of a well’s opening are measured in arshins.
- **Assassin** — one of the trades of the [[teni|esfera of Shadow]]. An assassin’s bracelet, like a pirate’s, grants impunity in the Old Port. See [[braslet]].
- **Atlas of Wells** — the [[gildiya-rybakov|Fishermen’s Guild’s]] book: a map of all known schönbruns, with landowners’ names. A master’s secretary must know it by heart.

## B
- **White, gray, and black posts** — posts at district boundaries: white indicates white land, gray indicates limited permission to commit offenses, and black indicates black land. See [[belaya-zemlya]].
- **Bargot** — an Ollandian city on the River Khursla; the Dangarian field marshal orders the army to besiege it. See [[bargot]], [[khursla]].
- **White land** — a district without crime: no locks, bars, or guards. White posts stand at crossroads, and its rules are upheld more by morality than by law. See [[belaya-zemlya]].
- **White-land murder** — murder on white land. It is Catalia’s gravest crime: the punishment is terrible, and such cases are almost unheard of in Laerta.
- **Bonk** — see **Almagarian bonk**.
- **Brante** — flying trees that grow in a grove on Solanta. Airships cannot be built without them. See [[solanta]], [[tekhnika]].
- **Bracelet** — a sign of esfera and rank, awarded by the [[palata-slova|Chamber of the Word]] after a dueling debate. An obrero has the lowest bracelet; a maestro of art wears silver, and above that is gold, the bracelet of a don. There are also wedding, friendship, and work bracelets, such as the green one worn by a hired driver. See [[braslet]].
- **Foreman** — the senior slave in a Dangarian dugout. He occupies the best bunk beside the stove and eats a triple portion. See [[brigadir]].
- **Brega** — an industrial Catalian city on the River Monho, with the largest coal deposits. Steam-powered machines and engines come from there. See [[brega]].
- **Brunnen** — Dangarian for an ordinary well containing water, rather than a schönbrun. Many are left by failed attempts. See [[kolodets]].

## V
- **Valentian Gardens** — a white district on Laerta’s outskirts; Doctor Juanes lived there. See [[khuanes]].
- **Valentian dynasty** — the former rulers of Laertar. The dynasty fell, its palace became a museum and restaurants, and the Bridge Bastion became an exhibition of instruments of torture.
- **Valisyan** — a drink favored by Count [[lurd|von Lurd]]. Katrina advises Dietrich to ask for it instead of schnapps.
- **Great Scroll** — a repository of records of debates and their outcomes; in it, “Truth” is the result of a judicial debate. Scribes work in glass booths, and records cannot be forged. Catalia has no taxes, and the names of creators of masterpieces are entered separately in the Scroll. See [[svitok]].
- **Promissory note** — a debt document with a payment deadline. Ramires’s attorney agrees to accept Alvaro’s note for a term of no more than three months. See [[dengi]].
- **The Herald** — a Laerta newspaper that publishes novel reviews and news of riots. See [[vestnik]].
- **Lords of the Abyss** — monstrous deities of the sea. Catalians believe they created the sea and the land. Later they came to be called the Ancients. See [[drevnie]].
- **Free people** — in Dangar, people who are free rather than enslaved. They feed stones porridge in church.
- **High Table** — Catalia’s governing body: thirty-three allens, three from each of the eleven esferas. Each year, the Table votes to expel its worst member and elects the supremo. See [[vysokij-stol]].
- **Wunde** — Hofstadt’s river. In spring it floods half a verst wide; at the end of summer it dries up for a month. See **Gehai**, [[khofshtadt]].

## G
- **Gehai** — the sacred month when the River Wunde dries up. Dangarians believe God is happy at this time, while clean water costs as much as wine. See [[khofshtadt]].
- **Fishermen’s Guild** — the association of masters who know how to “fish” in wells. Led by the magnus, it maintains the Atlas of Wells and owns slaves who dig. See [[gildiya-rybakov]], [[fishmajster]].
- **Grand Opera** — Laerta’s principal theater, where Señorita Violet is performed. See [[grand-opera]].
- **Ledger** — the book recording each digger’s Zustand; the conditions must be followed exactly. See [[tsushtand]].
- **Mushroom patch (mycelium)** — a slave settlement in the forest around the master’s residence: a church, the residence, and dugouts covered with earth up to their roofs. See [[red|Red]].
- **Hussars** — Ollandian cavalry, the flower of the principalities’ armies. A hussar squadron attacks [[ditrikh|Dietrich’s]] battery. See [[tri-zalpa]].

## D
- **Darinean Range** — a mountain range on the route northwest from Laerta; the railway to Selezia crosses its White Bone Pass. See [[seleziya]].
- **Darreggio** — the mirn of mechanics and engineering; takes over from the mirna [[vinsenya|Vinsenya]] at three in the afternoon. See [[darredzho]].
- **Orderly** — an officer’s servant. Dietrich’s orderly is the stone slave [[milosh|Milosh]].
- **Doge** — the ruler of Solanta. See [[solanta]], [[solantijskaya-ekspeditsiya]].
- **Don, doña** — a title for a leader of an esfera drawn from its own members. It is not inherited and is confirmed through a debate in the Chamber of the Word. In the esfera of Production, the “dons” are factory owners. A don’s wife is called doña. See [[braslet]].
- **Ancients** — the Catalian sea gods. People are considered their “proud creation,” and they are invoked in curses. Later they are called the Lords of the Abyss. See [[drevnie]].
- **Dueling debate** — Catalia’s principal means of settling conflicts. Instead of weapons, opponents compete with arguments before an arbiter and jury; money, a bracelet, or even a life may be at stake. See [[duelnyj-spor]], [[duelnyj-spor-ramiresa]].

## Ye
- **One God** — the faith of the [[muaris|Muari Emirates]]: an entire continent, fragmented into emirates, is united by a common belief.

## Zh
- **Toad Eye** — the nickname of one of the slave soldiers who guarded Red: he has bulging eyes and communicates by gestures. See [[red]].

## Z
- **Hares** — the middle stratum of Dangarian society: merchants, managers, and people in service. An eagle cannot serve under a hare. See [[orly]].
- **Fishing mirror** — spectators at ritual fishing look into the well through a mirror rather than directly, seeing an image of another world at the bottom. See [[rybalka-valbrekht]].
- **Golden Quill** — [[beatris|Beatriz’s]] nickname: at the Academy of Arts, she writes with a gold fountain pen.
- **Sorge um dich** — the Dangarian formula “I care for you,” spoken by free people as they feed slaves porridge. It is linked to the Sower’s commandment. See **feeding**.

## I
- **Hidalgo** — a knight. Before the First Judge, hidalgos were the main force in war. See [[lid-anteodor]].
- **Iolanta** — the mirna of Law, a celebrated debater. Alguacils wear her white feather. See [[iolanta]].
- **Truth** — in Catalia, whatever has been established by judicial debate and entered in the Great Scroll. See [[svitok]].

## K
- **Catalia the Mighty and Catalia the Beautiful** — the two parts of the country, separated by a mountain range. The Mighty lies in the northeast, with mines and factories; the Beautiful lies in the south, with the capital, coastline, and theaters. See [[kataliya]].
- **Kabarda** — the steppes on the way to the Ollandian border. See [[indra]].
- **Kadris** — a strong drink used to revive people who have fainted. See [[fernan-gerejra]].
- **Kaiser** — the emperor of Dangar. His word stands above the army and the Claws, but two other centers of power exist beside him. See **three centers of power**, [[dangar]].
- **State-commissioned raiders, privateers, corsairs, buccaneers** — types of pirates. Buccaneers operate along the coast, privateers on trade routes, state-commissioned raiders in wartime on behalf of a state, and corsairs raid cities. See [[karlos]].
- **Brand** — a mark on a slave’s body. Church slaves’ brands contain the Sower’s name and heal letter by letter; special slaves are branded with “Thou shalt not kill.”
- **Magnus’s clepsydra** — a golden water clock that slows time and muffles sounds around its owner. See [[amir-sedoj]].
- **Clarissa** — the mirna of life force, or vitality. People ask her for health.
- **Well** — in Catalia, “Well” with a capital letter means a miraculous well, called a schönbrun in Dangarian. There is usually white mist inside, with another world beyond it. See [[kolodets]].
- **Kaiser’s Claws** — Dangar’s secret service: counterintelligence and a special army department. They wear black uniforms with a silver falcon and answer directly to the Kaiser. See [[kogti-kajzera]].
- **Cornet** — a junior cavalry officer’s rank.
- **Feeding** — a Dangarian ritual: free people feed slaves porridge by hand while saying “Sorge um dich.” This is a privilege the stones won in the wars of the Three Kingdoms period, and it is considered a virtue. See [[kamni]], [[seyatel]].
- **Cofenado** — the Gereira family’s special drink: coffee with pepper and orange juice. It cures hangovers. See [[lyusil]].
- **Kobold Sea** — the sea in the northwest into which the Khursla flows; Dangar begins at its port. See [[khursla]], [[dangar]].

## L
- **Laerta** — Catalia’s capital, divided into eleven districts, one for each esfera. See [[laerta]].
- **Laertar and Catilez** — the ancient kingdoms from which Catalia later formed. The Valentian dynasty ruled Laertar.
- **Life Guards** — the Kaiser’s personal guard; its white standard bears the Sower’s face.
- **Latsen** — an Ollandian city at the country’s geographical center; after its capture, it became the Dangarian field marshal’s headquarters. See [[latsen]].
- **People of the word** — what native Catalians call themselves: their conflicts are settled by words rather than bullets. See [[kataliya]].

## M
- **Magnus** — head of the Fishermen’s Guild, one of Dangar’s three centers of power. See [[gildiya-rybakov]].
- **Mardur** — a Catalian-made 0.29-caliber revolver worn at the belt by the Dangarian “falcon.” See [[klyajn]].
- **Mark** — Dangar’s main monetary unit; the small coin is the pfennig. Ten-mark banknotes exist. See [[dengi]].
- **Maestro** — a master of a craft in the esfera of Art. A maestro’s bracelet is silver; gold is the next rank. See [[braslet]].
- **Master of law** — a person from the esfera of Law who argues in debates; wears a bracelet with a black snake if serving Fatima. See [[vinsente-ramires]].
- **Mergana** — a country on the River Khursla and birthplace of the philosopher Liess van Halm. It is described as the western edge of the known world. See [[mergana]], [[liess-van-khalm]].
- **Mershants** — a fortress on the border of Ollandia and Catalia. See [[mershants]].
- **Mirnas** — patron spirits of crafts and arts. In everyday life, people ask male and female mirnas for help as we might invoke muses; their statues adorn buildings. They are not gods, but stand above ordinary humans: according to belief, people become mirnas after death and judgment. See [[mirny]].
- **Muari** — inhabitants of the Muari Emirates, dark-skinned people who believe in one god. See [[muaris]].

## N
- **Insignia patches** — the Kaiser’s Claws’ distinctions: a patch with a silver falcon, with a hawk and red phoenix ranked above it. See [[kogti-kajzera]].
- **Kaiser’s governor** — an office sought in conquered Ollandia. A candidate is an Ollandian nobleman connected by marriage to the clergy or the military. See [[lents]].
- **Thou shalt not kill** — words branded onto some slaves’ chests with a red-hot chain. Such a person is incapable of killing.
- **Rifled weapons** — weapons with rifled barrels; Catalia’s revolvers and guns use them, outperforming Dangarian smoothbores. See [[tekhnika]].

## O
- **Oberfischmeister** — the higher rank of fisherman beyond master; can “take the catch along” and impose schönbrun effects on people. See [[landau]].
- **Obrero** — the lowest rank and a word for workers: servants, craftspeople, and everyone displaced by steam-powered machines. An obrero’s bracelet in the craft esfera is black and yellow; in the esfera of Production, obreros wear black. See [[obrero]].
- **Service** — the esfera of servants and minor occupations: nannies, barbers, cleaners, and copyists, whose booths stand beside workshops. Its emblem is a little shoe. See [[esfera]].
- **Ollandia** — a country of proud princes, hussars, and cathedrals, but without industry of its own: all its locomotives are Catalian. Dangar conquers it. See [[ollandiya]], [[olandskaya-vojna]].
- **Order of Diamond Hanna** — a high Dangarian decoration worn on a ribbon by Count von Wahrheit. Joriksen says that any general can “hang Saint Hanna around an officer’s neck” to buy him. See [[varkhajt]].
- **Order of Courage** — a Catalian award for heroism, also bestowed posthumously. See [[red|Rodrigo da Vira]].
- **Eagles** — Dangar’s nobility: people “with a von,” who serve out of a sense of honor. Landless eagles are poor but cannot serve under a hare. See [[orly]].
- **Ortiyar** — a fortress and junction thirty miles from Laerta, the “shield of the capital” and the country’s main railway hub. See [[ortiyar]].

## P
- **Steam-powered machine** — a machine that displaces obrero craftspeople, causing an unemployment crisis in Laerta. See [[tekhnika]], [[stachka-v-laerte]].
- **Bailiff** — an official of the Chamber of the Word who summons jurors. See [[palata-slova]].
- **Prosthesis** — a mechanical arm or leg, worn by engineers and rebels in Mor’s circle. See [[mor]], [[malysh]].
- **Customs duties** — absent in Laerta: trade is free, while smugglers export goods from other countries. See [[karlos]].
- **Chamber of the Word** — a cluster-shaped building without a single right angle, where dueling debates take place. See [[palata-slova]].
- **Patriarch** — head of the Church of the Sower and one of Dangar’s three centers of power. Influences the Kaiser. See [[seyatel]].
- **Peseta, peso** — small Catalian denominations alongside the escudo. See [[dengi]].
- **First Judge** — Catalia’s legendary lawgiver, creator of dueling debates and the Great Scroll. His principles are carved on slabs in the Scroll’s hall: “Skill is the key,” “What belongs to everyone belongs to no one,” “Honor matters more than morality,” and “One equals seven.” See [[pervyj-sudya]].
- **Jurors** — at least five people who either do not know the disputants or know both equally well. They receive no pay and attend the debate as if it were a free performance.
- **Pfennig** — a small Dangarian coin; Dietrich’s wife adds “not a pfennig” to the family reserve beyond the weekly limit. See [[dengi]].
- **Praiza (Preis)** — Dangarian for “prize”: what Yakka calls Greta as a reward for digging a well. See [[greta]].
- **Saint Adelia’s boarding school** — a school for young noblewomen in Hofstadt, where Olkha and Katrina studied.

## R
- **Right to divorce** — a limited right to divorce that can be used up: the chauffeur Martinez has “used up his right to divorce.” See [[ugo-martines]].
- **Reformists** — a secret society fighting for a just society; members wear yellow ribbons. They demand proportional representation of the esferas at the High Table. See [[reformisty]].
- **Fishing** — a schönbrun ritual: the master burns slips bearing wishes, the spectators look into the well, and they receive a “miracle.” The number of participants must be exact. See [[rybalka-valbrekht]].
- **Crafts (Craft)** — the old name for the esfera of Production; its emblem is a cogwheel. See [[esfera]].

## S
- **Airship** — a flying craft resembling a sailing ship. In Dangar, the patriarch’s golden airship flies over the city with bells and religious banners; in Catalia, airships are considered a prospect of the near future. See [[samolet]], [[tekhnika]].
- **The Glory of the Empire** — Count von Wahrheit’s Dangarian newspaper. See [[varkhajt]].
- **Silver bird** — the symbol above the entrance to a Dangarian church. See [[seyatel]].
- **Señor, señorita, ma donna** — Catalian forms of address. “Ma donna” is used for a woman of high rank, such as an allen. See [[alleny]].
- **Saint Hanna** — see **Order of Diamond Hanna**.
- **Scroll** — see **Great Scroll**.
- **Secunda** — an esfera’s second allen. See **allen**.
- **Sower** — the Dangarians’ god, who, according to tradition, created people and sowed them. A fresco shows him digging a well alone. His words are “Sorge um dich.” See [[seyatel]].
- **Gray land** — a district where theft and fighting are permitted, but killing and maiming are not; murder is tried in court there. See [[belaya-zemlya]].
- **Blue beyond** — the afterlife destination of worthy people. To “go into the blue beyond” means to die; the death of a good person is an occasion to congratulate their relatives. Its opposite is the abyss.
- **Sinkho** — a little tree planted outside the house of someone who has died. Ribbons on it express attitudes toward the deceased: blue means fond remembrance, red and black dislike or enmity. See [[sinkho]].
- **Solanta** — a small island amid reefs and currents, first allied with the Muari and then with Catalia. See [[solanta]], [[solantijskaya-ekspeditsiya]].
- **Falcon (black)** — a person wearing a falcon badge, an officer of the Kaiser’s Claws; in the slave settlement, an overseer. See [[klyajn]].
- **Old Port** — Laerta’s black district, a haven for thieves and pirates, where wearers of pirate and assassin bracelets have a free hand. See [[staryj-port]].
- **Supremo** — head of the High Table, elected every few years. See [[vysokij-stol]].
- **Señorita Violet** — an opera whose ending was rewritten at Filippe’s demand: the heroine stays alive. See [[filippe]], [[grand-opera]].

## T
- **Rattletrap** — the colloquial name for a self-propelled steam carriage. See [[tekhnika]].
- **Shadow (esfera)** — the esfera of criminal trades: thieves, pirates, assassins, and prostitutes. Permitted as long as it respects its limits. See [[teni]].
- **Dark schönbrun** — a well in which fishing is dangerous: it may bring sickness, misfortune, poverty, or fear. See [[temnyj-shyonbrun]].
- **Three Bulls** — the alliance of Craft, Agriculture, and Service — the bull is the farmers’ symbol — and the name of the tavern at their common boundary, the rebels’ headquarters. See [[tri-byka]].
- **Three centers of power** — in Dangar, the Kaiser with his army, the patriarch with his clergy, and the magnus of the Fishermen’s Guild. See [[dangar]].
- **Three Kingdoms** — a historical period in Dangar; in its wars, stones won the right to “care.” See [[kamni]].

## U
- **Skill is the key** — one of the First Judge’s principles. Beatriz repeats it on seeing contented peasants. See [[pervyj-sudya]].
- **Unsicher** — “unreliable”: the status of noblemen in a conquered country who face the loss of their lands. See [[dangar]].
- **Noncommissioned officer** — a junior commander in the Dangarian army, commanding a platoon.

## F
- **Weather vane** — a device that determines who speaks first at the start of a debate. See [[duelnyj-spor]].
- **Feldwebel, staff captain, lieutenant** — Dangarian army ranks. A battery’s commander is a staff captain. See [[ditrikh]].
- **Flamberto** — the mirn of pleasures. See [[mirny]].
- **Fischmeister** — “master-fish,” a fisherman who can catch a miracle in wells. Wears an azure cloak with a gold catfish brooch. See [[fishmajster]].
- **Musket** — a Dangarian smoothbore firearm without a front sight, fired once every minute and a half. See [[tekhnika]].

## Kh
- **Women’s hamlet** — a separate settlement attached to a slave camp, with huts for female slaves; the women themselves are called “prizes.” See [[greta]].
- **Khursla** — the great river on which steamships travel from Ollandia to Mergana. See [[khursla]].
- **Hofstadt** — Dangar’s capital, with two million inhabitants. See [[khofshtadt]].

## Ts
- **May your hand be steady** — a Dangarian military greeting. See [[dangar]].
- **Zustand** — the Dangarian word for “condition,” the circumstances under which a slave digs a well; each slave has an individual one. A special sentence is “kein Zustand,” the absence of an assignment. See [[tsushtand]].
- **Chain with a stone** — a slave’s emblem: a stone hangs from a chain around the neck, symbolizing God’s love. See [[kamni]].

## Ch
- **Cup of misfortunes** — a Catalian belief: the mirna Eliga has measured out a cup of troubles for each person, with one great misfortune at the bottom. See [[eliga]].
- **Black Maiden** — the Muari deity invoked by the sorcerer Faid-Musa. See [[lid-anteodor]].
- **Black land** — a dangerous district where everything is allowed. See [[belaya-zemlya]].
- **Black ribbons** — a sign of a suspicious death on a sinkho tree. See [[sinkho]].
- **Strangers** — beings living beyond the mist in a well. See [[kolodets]].

## Sh
- **Schönbrun** — Dangarian for “Beautiful Well”: a well containing a miracle. Mist conceals its bottom, and another world is visible beyond it. See [[kolodets|Wells]].
- **Schlissel** — “key”: Hofstadt’s district containing the ministries, palace, and patriarchal cathedral. See [[khofshtadt]].
- **Stein** — “stone,” a slave in Dangar. See [[kamni]].
- **Stoss** — a card game played at Baroness Walbrecht’s.

## E
- **Eikha** — Yakka’s gun: he believes a soul lives within it and that a charm prevents the gun from killing its owner. See [[ejkha]].
- **Eyring** — an ancient Dangarian family and a town. See [[ejring]].
- **Eliga** — the mirna of luck, a two-faced female spirit. Red has a tattoo of her with the faces of a girl and a serpent. See [[eliga]].
- **Esfera** — a Catalian estate united by occupation. There are eleven esferas, three large and eight small. See [[esfera]].
- **Escudo** — the principal Catalian coin; a thousand escudos is a large sum. See [[dengi]].
- **Esperanza** — a brand of steam engines and Mor’s factory. See [[esperantsa]].

## Ya
- **Yard** — a measure of depth: wells are measured in yards. See [[tsushtand]].
