**Eikha** is the gun belonging to [[yakka|Yakka]], a hunter and assistant to Master [[joriksen|Joriksen]]. To Yakka, she is more than a weapon: he believes that Eikha has a soul, and he talks to her.

## Appearance (ch. 4–8)
Red first sees the gun on his escort’s back in ch. 4: a double-barreled flintlock, old but very beautiful, with a carved stock and silver patterns on the fore-end. In his thoughts, Red calls it an “old flintlock lady.” Yakka strokes the gun when he smokes, when he waits, and when he tells a long story in Dangarian. Red sees this at times as a threat, at times as misplaced tenderness. In ch. 8, he runs his finger along the barrel and the silver pattern, and Yakka does not object. The gun shoots accurately in Yakka’s hands: he knocks a pine cone off a stump at twenty paces.

Yakka does not willingly part with his weapon: during the church service in ch. 8, he stands by the wall “as though naked without his beloved gun,” because he has had to leave it behind.

## The gun in Red’s hands (ch. 12–15)
In ch. 12, Red knocks Yakka unconscious on the way to Greta, takes his gun, powder, bullets, fire steel, and gloves, and escapes from Dunkwald. In ch. 15, Yakka follows his trail without fearing a shot from ambush: the gun is under a charm, and the ancestral spirits will not let Eikha’s bullet kill her master. When he finds Red unconscious, the first thing he does is check the gun to see whether the Catalian has taken her soul. The soul is still there. He then says that if Red had stolen Eikha’s soul, Yakka would have left him to freeze, but Red acted wisely.

It was with Eikha that Red fought off the wolves during his escape. Having recovered the gun, Yakka lays it across his knees in relief and lights his pipe.

## Eikha as an adviser (ch. 23, 33)
In ch. 23, while being questioned by Baron [[landau|Landau]], Yakka strokes the gun as he replies that he serves only himself, not the fischmeister. In ch. 33, he decides to leave Dunkwald: the Zustands have become too bad. When Red asks where he will go, Yakka strokes the gun and says that he may hunt deer, and that he will “consult Eikha—we’ll decide together.”

## The concept and interviews
The author did not mention Eikha specifically in interviews. In recounting the plot, the stream’s host noted that Yakka rescued the fugitive Red in winter and did not resent the fact that Red had struck him and taken his gun. This is the host’s opinion, not the author’s words.
