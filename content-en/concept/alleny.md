Allen is the highest position within an esfera: a representative of its interests at the [[vysokij-stol|High Table]]. Each of the eleven [[esfera|esferas]] has exactly three allens regardless of its population, which is why the allens of the Three Bulls are considered disadvantaged compared with those of the smaller esferas (ch. 1, 7).

## What is known about allens (ch. 3–34)
- **Prim-allen.** In the novel, this is the title of an esfera’s leading allen: [[filippe|Filippe di Berro]] is prim-allen of Art (ch. 3), and [[fatima|Fatima Ir-Reya]] is prim-allen of Law (ch. 28). Her bracelet is white gold with a diamond feather of the mirna [[iolanta|Iolanta]]. Chapter 22 also mentions the prim-allens of Law and Shadow, whom [[karlos|Carlos el Niña]] knows.
- **Influence.** Fatima, for example, oversees the legal professionals of Law whom the factory owners hire; Mor believes that for the Reformists to win, they must persuade Fatima to forbid her lawyers from representing the factory owners (ch. 20). General [[dezerej|Deserey]] addresses her as “ma donna,” a formula used when requesting a great favor (ch. 28).
- **Vulnerability.** Each year, the Table expels its worst allen. Filippe has held his place for six years (ch. 3); Fatima fears that she will not be spared during the purge (ch. 28).
- **The ambitious.** The industrialist [[kristobal-maduro|Maduro]] aspires to become an allen (ch. 34); in ch. 13, Alvaro demands that only one of Craft’s three allens should be a factory owner.

## Allens in the story (ch. 3, 24–28)
[[alvaro-da-silva|Alvaro]] happens to become Fatima’s lover without knowing that she is an allen of Law (ch. 24–28). When he finds out, he accuses her of deceiving him; Fatima replies that she knew about him from the beginning and was checking whether he was Filippe’s spy. The commission for a book about the heroes of Solanta comes from Filippe (ch. 3). See also [[vysokij-stol]], [[esfera]].
