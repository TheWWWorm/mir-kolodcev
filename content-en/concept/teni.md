The esfera of Shadow is a Catalian institution that would astonish foreigners. In most countries, criminals are caught and executed; in Catalia, they form a full-fledged [[esfera|esfera]] with their own traditions, heraldry, and representation at the [[vysokij-stol|High Table]]. [[alvaro-da-silva|Alvaro]] lectures on this in ch. 9.

## Rules (ch. 9–18)
- **Districts.** Catalians allow criminals to exist freely, and in exchange Shadow prevents arbitrary violence and wrongdoing. There is no crime on white land; minor offenses are allowed on grey land—theft, fraud, and fistfights, but not murder—and any offense is allowed on black land ([[belaya-zemlya]]).
- **Each according to their trade.** Masters of Shadow do not commit just any villainy, but act according to their trades: thieves steal, swindlers deceive, assassins kill, and pirates rob foreigners. Many trades are useful to the country. According to Alvaro’s lecture, pirates plunder foreign ships and ports, flooding Catalia with overseas goods.
- **The law of the trade.** Prostitution is an ordinary trade of Shadow, alongside those of the thief, robber, and boarding fighter; it is legal in Laerta because any occupation that brings benefit or joy is legal in Catalia (ch. 12).
- **Information.** Shadow’s law permits trading in information: damaging information about notables circulates like bank checks and can serve as collateral with a moneylender (ch. 18).

## Shadow’s guards (ch. 13)
Two kinds of people maintain order in Catalia. Guards belong to the esfera of Shadow: thick beards without mustaches, a red scarf around their hair in pirate fashion, and whatever weapons are at hand. They intervene when the fact of a crime is obvious: a fight in the wrong place, violence against a woman who is not wearing a prostitute’s bracelet, or a master of Shadow exceeding the authority of their guild. Alguacils, by contrast, come from the esfera of Law and investigate according to the “letter of the Scroll.”

## Shadow in the story (ch. 7–30)
- **Carlos el Niña.** A smuggler and former corsair who has earned the highest bracelet of the esfera of Shadow. He lives in a former bastion above the Old Port, a black district where someone with a pirate’s or assassin’s bracelet may kill with impunity (ch. 7). The envelope containing the “buccaneer’s” reply is marked with the crossed knives of the esfera of Shadow.
- **Clashes.** Shadow’s thugs join the guards in beating obreros outside [[balboa|Balboa’s]] arms factory (ch. 11). Maduro prepares for a bloodbath and recruits hundreds of members of Shadow (ch. 30). Shadow is part of the war party (ch. 26).
- **A white murder.** Alvaro specifically wants to seek a don of Shadow’s advice to understand how murder could be committed on white land (ch. 18). Carlos explains that blood is not spilled on white sand: Catalia tolerates the people of Shadow as long as they know their limits.
- **Red.** In Dangar, Red realizes that the nighttime condition in his [[tsushtand|Zustand]] is a reference to the esfera of Shadow (ch. 15).

## Foreigners and Shadow (ch. 17, 18)
For other states, Shadow is the principal headache: pirate schooners prowl the seas (ch. 17). Around forty foreigners sailed under Carlos’s flag, and not one shed blood on white land: a foreigner would be the first suspect, and the punishment is terrible (ch. 18).
