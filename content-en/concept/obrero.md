Obreros are the ordinary workers of the [[esfera|esferas]]: factory workers, peasants, servants, and small-scale craftsmen. The word is spoken differently in different places: with pride in the Three Bulls tavern, but as a stigma in the restaurant near the Grand Opera, where [[alvaro-da-silva|Alvaro]] first encounters this contempt (ch. 1). The first thing the reader sees is Alvaro suddenly ashamed of having brought two obreros into a restaurant for people of Art and Law. Alvaro’s friends [[fernan-gerejra|Fernan]] and [[lyusil|Lucille]] belong to Service, and are therefore obreros.

## Status (ch. 1–20)
- **Rank.** The obrero’s bracelet is the lowest. An obrero of Craft wears a black-and-yellow bracelet (ch. 5); ordinary obreros wear a plain black bracelet without symbols (ch. 20).
- **Mobility.** According to a speaker in the Three Bulls tavern, changing esferas is almost impossible: the dons and maestros have taken the easy jobs, while the obreros are left with the plow and workbench. He says that obreros are kept “under the saddle, not in it” (ch. 5).
- **Representation.** The Three Bulls hold nine of the thirty-three seats at the [[vysokij-stol|High Table]], although these esferas contain the great majority of obreros (ch. 1, 7).
- **The chance to rise.** According to the proverb “Skill is the key,” any street sweeper or shepherd can become a don if they prove their skill in the Chamber of the Word (ch. 7). The Reformists point out that in practice, only a few succeed.

## Crisis and strike (ch. 0–30)
At the beginning of the book, newspaper boys shout that steam-powered machinery is displacing obreros and that mass layoffs are taking place in [[laerta|Laerta]] (ch. 0). The obreros challenge the factory owners’ right to dismiss workers without cause in the [[palata-slova|Chamber of the Word]], but the owners send legal professionals from the esfera of Law in their place and win (ch. 7, 11). Afterward, the obreros go to [[balboa|Balboa’s]] factory, where guards and members of Shadow beat them.

[[mor|Mor]], an engineer of the highest rank in the esfera of Production, showed a way out. Ten years earlier, in Brega, she and other workers who had lost their jobs founded their own factory, Esperanza, planned for two thousand people: the obreros mortgaged their homes, while Mor promised the bankers of the esfera of Finance a share of the profits. Now that the crisis has reached Laerta, the obreros ask for her help. With the help of legal professionals from Law, the factory owners challenge preferential loans for workers in the Chamber of the Word and win (ch. 20). The unrest grows into a strike, and the High Table demands that the allens of Production restore order (ch. 28, 30). See [[stachka-v-laerte]], [[reformisty]].

## The concept
In interviews, the author described a Catalian’s career path from ordinary worker to maestro and don. In the transcript of his remarks, the first rank is written as “arera”; it corresponds to “obrero” only in meaning.
