Esferas are the foundation of [[kataliya|Catalia’s]] social order. Unlike traditional estates, they are defined by trade rather than birth: every Catalian belongs to the esfera whose work they do and wears a bracelet showing their esfera and rank ([[braslet]]). In the novel, the esferas are seen through the eyes of the writer [[alvaro-da-silva|Alvaro da Silva]], who belongs to the esfera of Art.

## Organization
There are eleven esferas. Three are enormous: Craft, later called the esfera of Production, Agriculture, and Service. They are known as the “Three Bulls” ([[tri-byka]]) because they feed, clothe, and serve the country and produce almost all its exports. The other eight have fewer people and produce barely a tenth of the goods. They are named as Law, Knowledge, Shadow, Transport, Medicine, War, Finance, and Art. Ordinary workers call the smaller esferas “parasites,” because the usefulness of some of them is difficult to understand.

Politics rests on the esferas: at the [[vysokij-stol|High Table]], each is represented by three [[alleny|allens]], no more and no less, so the Three Bulls hold nine of the thirty-three seats, while the eight smaller esferas hold twenty-four. This underlies the complaints of the Reformists and obreros ([[obrero]], [[reformisty]]): the esfera of Art has about a hundred thousand members, while the agricultural esfera has at least six million, yet they have equal votes.

Each esfera governs itself. A Catalian proverb says: “A tree grows on its own roots.” The High Table handles foreign policy and relations between esferas but does not interfere in their internal affairs: no one except the farmers will punish a farmer, tax him, or help him. Internal affairs are managed by dons who come from the esfera itself. A second proverb is “Skill is the key”: no one is born a don; a don’s son receives only money and knowledge, not a gold bracelet, while the title must be earned and confirmed in a disputation in the Chamber of the Word ([[duelnyj-spor]], [[palata-slova]]).

## Ranks
Ranks within an esfera are distinguished by bracelets. The ranks named in the book are obrero (the lowest), master, maestro, and don. Alvaro’s first bracelet is the silver bracelet of a maestro of Art; he dreams of a gold one and being addressed as “don.” According to the author in an interview, the Catalian “dream” is to climb this ladder through one’s own labor, like the American dream; the transcript gives the steps as “arera → maestro → don,” where the first word apparently means obrero. In the author’s words, Catalia is not a state “in our sense”: each esfera is more like a federation, society is individualistic, and everyone is concerned with personal success.

## The esferas in turn (ch. 1–30)
- **Art.** It keeps nocturnal hours: eight in the morning is “the middle of the night” for this esfera (ch. 9). Alvaro belongs to it; a don of Art is [[filippe|Filippe di Berro]].
- **Law.** The esfera of judges, legal professionals, and [[algvasily|alguacils]], distinguished by the white feather of the mirna [[iolanta|Iolanta]] in their hats. The arbiter of a dueling debate always wears a bracelet of Law. The prim-allen of Law is [[fatima|Fatima Ir-Reya]]; her lawyers are called “snakes,” after the black snake on their bracelets (ch. 1).
- **Shadow.** The esfera of criminal trades; it has its own article, [[teni]].
- **War.** The military; the allens of War include Field Marshal Cortago and others. In ch. 28, General [[dezerej|Deserey]] asks for the issue of mobilization to be raised.
- **Production (Craft).** Formerly called Craft, a name preserved in Craftsmen’s Square with its temple of Darreggio (ch. 30). Factory owners are the dons of this esfera. Mor is an engineer of the highest rank, with a black bracelet bearing three gold gears.
- **Finance.** Bankers; they support the obreros against the factory owners in ch. 11, 20.
- **Transport, Medicine, Knowledge.** Smaller esferas, named in the book with almost no detail. Red dreams of transferring to Medicine and opening a healing well (ch. 23).
- **Service and Agriculture.** Together with Craft, they form the Three Bulls. Service includes nannies such as [[lyusil|Lucille]], copyists, and others.

## Moving between esferas (ch. 5)
Changing esferas is almost impossible, complains a speaker wearing the bracelets of an obrero of Craft in the Three Bulls tavern: “they”—the dons—have taken the easy jobs, while the plow and workbench are left to the obreros (ch. 5). The various esferas have their own districts in Laerta, one for each; the Three Bulls tavern stands where the districts of Craft, Service, and Agriculture meet.

## Conflicts between esferas in the story (ch. 7–28)
Clashes between esferas set the Catalian storyline in motion. Steam-powered machinery displaces workers; the obreros challenge the factory owners to a debate in the Chamber of the Word, and the factory owners send legal professionals from the esfera of Law in their place. The obreros lose, are beaten by Shadow mercenaries outside Balboa’s factory, and a strike begins (ch. 7, 11, 20). Meanwhile, the general asks Fatima to raise mobilization before the High Table, while the parties of war and peace compete for influence there (ch. 26, 28). See [[stachka-v-laerte]].

## Laerta’s districts (ch. 5, 9)
The capital is divided into eleven districts, one for each esfera. Where the districts of Craft, Service, and Agriculture meet stands the Three Bulls tavern, with three emblems on its walls: a little shoe, a gear, and a bull’s head. The surrounding land is grey ([[belaya-zemlya]]), and people here like to discuss justice and the reform of the High Table. The esfera of Art keeps nocturnal hours: eight in the morning is “the middle of the night” for it (ch. 9). People of Art and Law dine at restaurants near the Grand Opera; the Old Port ([[staryj-port]]) remains the black district set aside for the trades of [[teni|Shadow]].

## War and the esferas (ch. 26–28)
On the question of war with Dangar, the esferas act as political parties. The war party consists of generals, members of Shadow, and some of the allens of Medicine, Service, and Transport; the peace party consists of merchants, scholars, metal producers, and mine owners (ch. 26). General [[dezerej|Deserey]] asks Fatima to raise mobilization: at least two hundred thousand unemployed people and former soldiers should be transferred into the esfera of War, at a cost of three hundred million escudos in the first month and a hundred million in each subsequent month. By his reckoning, Catalia has fifteen battle-ready divisions against fifty Dangarian ones (ch. 28). The money would have to come from the unpopular “Three Bulls.”

## Mirnas and esferas
Each field of activity has its own mirna, a patroness or “muse.” At the Academy of Arts, time is marked by figurines of mirnas on a circular balcony: when the mirna of fertility, [[vinsenya|Vinsenya]], emerges beneath the enameled sun, it is about two in the afternoon (ch. 3). The mirn of mechanics, [[darredzho|Darreggio]], is the patron of craftsmen and engineers (ch. 5, 30), while the mirna [[iolanta|Iolanta]] is patroness of lawyers and keepers of the law, symbolized by the white feather in an alguacil’s hat. Catalians ask them for inspiration as we ask it of muses. According to the author, every field of activity has mirnas, not just art; they are not gods, they are not worshipped, and there is no proof that they ever existed. See [[mirny]].

## An esfera as destiny (ch. 1, 13)
Membership of an esfera determines where someone may dine, whom they are equal to, and what disputes they have the right to pursue. In ch. 1, an unfamiliar gentleman at the next table remarks that the restaurant near the Grand Opera is intended for the higher ranks of Art and Law, yet [[alvaro-da-silva|Alvaro]] has brought two obreros; Alvaro realizes with acute shame that his friends are not his equals in rank. This scene sets the plot in motion: the argument about “one equaling seven” shows that rank carries weight within the law itself. In ch. 13, the same Alvaro, visiting the industrialist Maduro’s people, states his program: of the three allens of the esfera of Craft, only one should be a factory owner, while the other two should come from the ranks of obreros and masters.

## The concept
The author stressed that the system of interaction between esferas has an economic side that he will show separately, and that “Catalia is not Spain”: its style is Spanish, but its structure is entirely different.
