**Darreggio** is the [[mirny|mirn]]—the masculine form—of mechanics, or, as the exhibition puts it, engineering. He is the patron of mechanics, in keeping with the location of his temple in the craftsmen’s square.

## On the academy clock (ch. 5)
On the tower of the [[akademiya-iskusstv|Academy of Arts]], Darreggio’s figurine emerges beneath an enameled sun at three in the afternoon, replacing [[vinsenya|Vinsenya]]. The narrator ironically calls him a lazy fellow.

## Darreggio in a painting (ch. 16)
At the “Philosophy of Horror” exhibition in the [[galereya-eliste|Eliste Gallery]], one painting shows Darreggio cutting open his own belly and inserting a miniature steam engine. His extracted intestines lie beside him, with flies circling above them. The caption reads: “The highest power is power over oneself.” [[filippe|Filippe di Berro]] advises Alvaro to think about it.

## The temple in Craftsmen’s Square (ch. 30)
A grey-stone temple of Darreggio stands at the center of Laerta’s Craftsmen’s Square. It forces the flow of people around the edges of the square. Two-story houses crowd its perimeter, each containing a workshop or shop: a shoemaker’s, a hardware shop, or a coppersmith’s. The narrator says that the square recalls a time when the city was sustained by small family workshops, without monopolies.
