**Esperanza** is the name given to two things in the novel: a factory in [[brega|Brega]] and the wheeled steam carriages powered by its engines. In the preface (ch. 0), newspaper boys shout about “the new technological marvel—Esperanza engines,” and in the chapters that follow, [[beatris|Beatriz]] is already driving such a carriage.

## Beatriz’s car (ch. 9–34)
Beatriz meets Fernan beside a red lacquered carriage. She says it is an Esperanza with a new compact engine from Brega (ch. 9). On the road, she drives very fast, puts on goggles, and overtakes light carriages and stagecoaches so quickly that their drivers barely have time to curse the “smoky rattletrap” (ch. 22). This car carries the protagonists to [[ortiyar|Ortiyar]], the coast, the Great Scroll, and the city (ch. 9, 11, 20, 22, 24). In the finale, Beatriz rides in the Esperanza with Fernan, while Alvaro returns with Fatima (ch. 34).

## The factory in Brega (ch. 20, 26)
The factory’s history is told by [[mor|Mor]], Esperanza’s chief engineer and the owner of a two-percent share in the enterprise.
- Ten years earlier, Brega suffered an unemployment crisis. Mor, the inventor of the pressure condenser, brought the dismissed workers together.
- They challenged the dons of industry to a debate, but lost to the argument “whoever owns the machines makes the rules.” They then decided to pool their resources and open a factory of their own, with two thousand participants.
- Construction was financed by credit: the workers mortgaged their homes, and Mor promised the bank a share of future profits. The factory began operating three years later, and became profitable a year after that.
- The pressure condenser made it possible to build a very compact steam engine for a light wheeled vehicle. These vehicles are sold even in the Muari Emirates.
- When a similar crisis struck [[laerta|Laerta]], the dons of industry did not want new competitors to emerge. They hired legal professionals and won a debate against the bankers in the [[palata-slova|Chamber of the Word]]: preferential loans to workers were ruled to be interference in the affairs of the esfera of Production, and banks can no longer issue them (ch. 20).

In ch. 26, Alvaro hears from an unknown woman that Esperanza has given the factory owners competition: its engines are better and cheaper than those of Iturbe-Clemento, so the other esferas would benefit from Mor’s victory. Seven factory owners, including [[kristobal-maduro|Maduro]], have monopolized weapons and steam engines.

## Advertising
Mor shows Alvaro the newspaper “[[vestnik|The Herald]]”: the only thing in it that is true is the Esperanza advertisement on the last page. The advertisement offers compact engines for fast vehicles, “the future of technology” (ch. 20).
