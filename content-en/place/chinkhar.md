Chinkhar is a northern land that [[dangar|Dangar]] conquered in the past and that now answers to the Kaiser. On the author’s map, its name is written in italics among the mountains in the northwestern part of Dangarian territory.

## History (ch. 10)
Seventy years ago, during the Chinkhar Wars, Empress Augusta looked after officers’ widows. But the war dragged on, producing widows faster than houses could be built, and the empress eventually made peace. She never started another war. The conquest of Chinkhar was completed only thirty years later, under her grandson. The capital’s Witwen district preserves the memory of those wars ([[khofshtadt|Hofstadt]]).

## The Chinkharians (ch. 14, 27)
In chapter 14, the narrator identifies an aquiline nose, sharply defined cheekbones, and intent green eyes as distinctive features of a Chinkharian northerner—features like [[joriksen|Joriksen’s]]. Three generations ago, people like him “gave people like [[ditrikh|von Dort]] a hard time,” but then they accepted the Kaiser’s rule, moved to the capital, and became fishermen. Chapter 27 says that Fischmeister Fritjof has “some Chinkharian blood,” which is considered a blemish in his ancestry: this is precisely why he was sent to serve in [[dunkvald|Dunkwald]]. In an interview, Joriksen was compared to a member of a “Scandinavian” people ethnically close to the Dangarians, and the empire to a state assimilating its conquered population; the author did not object.
