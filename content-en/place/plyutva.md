Plyutva is a city in western [[ollandiya|Ollandia]], where the railway line to [[latsen|Latsen]] and [[mershants|Mershants]] begins. On the author’s map, it is labeled right beside the Dangarian border.

## The fall of Plyutva (ch. 2, 10)
At the beginning of the novel, Plyutva has already fallen: the war has moved east, while Lieutenant [[ditrikh|Dietrich’s]] battery remains behind to guard the junction in the rear. Dietrich recalls praying during the siege to become an officer rather than remain a common eagle without land or stones. Later (ch. 10), we learn that Plyutva was bombarded, that “half the city was blown apart,” and that ten thousand Ollandians were branded as stones. Reports in chapter 31 describe it as “destroyed almost to its foundations.”

## The railway (ch. 28–31)
The Plyutva–Mershants line is the only railway supplying the Dangarian army; the Catalian general [[dezerej|Deserey]] learns that the line between Plyutva, Latsen, and Mershants is under construction (ch. 28). Dietrich’s first battle took place at the hundredth verst of the Plyutva–Latsen line (ch. 29). The voivodeship to which the city belongs is called the Plyutva Voivodeship.
