Selezia is a province of [[kataliya|Catalia]]. On the author’s map, a humorous note beside its name calls it “the land of rice fields and dreary ballads.” In chapter 4, the narrator describes it as vast seas of rice with little islands of villages and the beacons of small churches, inducing drowsiness; chapter 16 calls it “a dreary land of ruins and rice fields.”

## Geography and everyday life
The train from [[laerta|Laerta]] heads northwest through the Darineian Range and the White Bone Pass, then crosses Selezia (ch. 4). Faramo station is built from soot-grey stone left over from a count’s castle destroyed in the Third War of Kings; one castle tower still stands, resembling a broken tooth. The plateau is cool, with snow in winter (ch. 32). One colorful detail is that Alvaro dines on paella to the sound of Selezian ballads (ch. 1)—the “Selezian ballad” is a recognized musical or song genre in the novel. Among [[red|Red’s]] scars is a mark left by a “Selezian dagger” (ch. 12); the text does not make clear whether this refers to the province or to the country of Seleza.

## Van Halm and Tres Molinos (ch. 16, 26, 32, 34)
The philosopher [[liess-van-khalm|Liess van Halm]], a native of [[mergana|Mergana]], settled in Selezia after buying an estate on the plateau. Chapter 16 stresses the oddness of his choice: a foreigner who chose this dull region apparently did understand the Catalian soul. In chapter 32, the characters travel to Selezia on the Laerta–Seleza train and get off at [[tres-molinos|Tres Molinos]] station, a hundred miles short of the city of Seleza; [[posledniaya-zima|Last Winter]] Castle stands nearby.
