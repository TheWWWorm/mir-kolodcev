The Khursla is a navigable river on which stand [[ginarva|Ginarva]], [[bargot|Bargot]], and the capital of [[mergana|Mergana]]. It is labeled in light blue on the author’s map, with its western continuation shown as a dotted line extending beyond the depicted land.

## The river journey (ch. 4)
From Ginarva, the reader travels downstream for three days aboard a low-sided steamship. Bands stand at every landing, and a border bastion overlooks a bend in the river. Beyond Ollandia, the river runs through Mergana, where the traveler goes ashore at the Professors’ Bridge. Farther on, it reaches the Kobold Sea, where a [[dangar|Dangarian]] port has strict inspections and coastal forts.

## A wartime barrier (ch. 31)
The Dangarian command planned to supply its southern army by steamship, via the sea and the Khursla. At the beginning of summer, [[kurtsleben|Kurtzleben]] brought his troops to the river, but the steamships failed to arrive: part of the route crossed Mergana’s territory, and Mergana refused the vessels passage. The foreign ministry resolved the issue, but the ships had already been reassigned to other routes. Now the army must cross the river through an outflanking maneuver carried out by [[khofmajer|Hofmeyer]], rather than a frontal assault.
