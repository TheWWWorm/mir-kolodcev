The Chamber of the Word is a building in [[laerta|Laerta]] where [[duelnyj-spor|dueling debates]] are resolved according to the code of the [[pervyj-sudya|First Judge]]. From outside, it is an “upturned bunch of grapes” without a single right angle, its “grape” halls varying in size; the text gives their number as thirty-one or thirty-seven. For more about its legal foundations, see [[kataliya|Catalia]].

## Procedure for a debate (ch. 1)
A debate requires an arbiter wearing a bracelet of the Law esfera, two disputants, and at least five jurors who know both parties equally well or neither of them. Jurors are unpaid and attend for the spectacle: the Chamber is called a “free theater.” A little weather vane decides who speaks first. A clerk records the opponent’s name, and a bailiff summons the jurors. In chapter 1, [[alvaro-da-silva|Alvaro]] debates [[vinsente-ramires|Vincente Ramires]] here ([[duelnyj-spor-ramiresa|The Dueling Debate with Ramires]]), and the arbiter observes that the debate must not be allowed to descend into truisms.

## Its role in society (ch. 7–22)
The Chamber of the Word settles every kind of conflict: divorce, workers’ rights, and entitlement to the title of don. The Chamber awards bracelets after an examination (ch. 12), so the verdict “the arguments are unquestionably compelling” is the highest possible assessment. Members of the Law esfera are hired as “fighters” in debates: in chapter 7, factory owners put forward hired representatives from the Law esfera and defeat obreros in a dispute over dismissals ([[obrero|obreros]]); in chapter 11, the obreros and bankers lose a debate. Debate outcomes are entered into the [[svitok|Great Scroll]].
