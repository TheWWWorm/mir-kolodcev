Ortiyar is a fortress and railway junction in a narrow valley, thirty miles from [[laerta|Laerta]]. Apart from one other pass, it is the only place where the railway networks of northeastern “Mighty” Catalia and southern “Beautiful” Catalia connect: streams of freight pass through the junction before spreading across the country. There is no trading town in the valley—there was no room for one—only a transshipment station and a fortress. On the author’s map, Ortiyar is marked as “Ortiyar Fortress,” with the nearby note “General Deserey serves here.”

## The fortifications (ch. 9)
The word “fortress” suggests high walls and drawbridges, but the narrator’s description of Ortiyar is quite different: a complex system of bastions and redoubts, scarps and counterscarps, concealed firing positions, and underground ammunition supply routes. It cannot be approached without coming under crossfire from at least three positions, and the military calls it the “shield of Laerta.” There is also a training ground: at the artillery range, [[alvaro-da-silva|Alvaro]] and his companions watch mortars, bombards, and canister shot being fired, try binoculars with rangefinding markings, and listen to the general explain linear and quadratic relationships in firepower.

## Deserey and the war (ch. 9, 28, 30)
In chapter 9, General [[dezerej|Gabriel Deserey]] invites Alvaro to Ortiyar, tears apart the battle scenes in his novel, and asks him to convince his readers that Dangar is dangerous. In chapter 28, he reports to [[fatima|Fatima]] on his inspection of Ortiyar, personnel changes, and the plan to modernize its fortifications; in chapter 30, he writes Alvaro a note mentioning Ortiyar as his posting.
