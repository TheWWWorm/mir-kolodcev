Ginarva is a town on the River [[khursla|Khursla]] in [[ollandiya|Ollandia]]. In chapter 4, its name is written “Girnava”; on the author’s map, it is “Ginarva.”

## The end of the tracks (ch. 4)
The reader traveling from Catalia reaches the end of the rails here. The only way to continue is to board a low-sided steamship that takes three days to make its way down the Khursla, greeted at every landing by a brass band. At a bend in the river stands a border bastion, which fires a farewell cannon shot; the steamer answers with its whistle. Beyond lies [[mergana|Mergana]] (ch. 4).
