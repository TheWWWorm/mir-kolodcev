Eyring is a city in [[dangar|Dangar]] named after the family that owns it. On the author’s map, a note beside it reads “the ancestral seat of those very von Eyrings”—a reference to chapter 17, where [[ditrikh|Dietrich]], learning that his cover story makes him [[katrina|Katrina von Eyring’s]] cousin, asks whether these are the Eyrings after whom the city is named. “The city is their hereditary possession,” [[olkha|Olkha]] replies.

## The von Eyring family (ch. 17, 25, 29, 31)
The Eyrings are an ancient and powerful family. Prince [[lents|Lenz]] is told that killing Lieutenant von Eyring over a trivial matter would earn him influential enemies at court, especially if the killer were a foreigner (ch. 25). In their cover story, Dietrich and Katrina are cousins from a cadet branch of the family. Its head is Margrave Maximian von Eyring, a miser who nonetheless protects his own (ch. 29); in chapter 31, the calling cards of Count [[lurd|Ludwig von Lurd]] and Margrave von Eyring help secure Dietrich’s release from the gendarmerie. The city itself is not described in the text.
