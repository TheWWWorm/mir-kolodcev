The Old Port is a district on the [[laerta|Laerta]] waterfront, identified in chapter 7 as “black”: the law permits wearers of pirate or assassin bracelets to commit almost any crime there. Posts divide the port: Alessina Bay, with the dons’ yachts; Christopher’s Port, with grey posts, where the law’s reach is limited; and the Old Port, with a black one. Murder here incurs a fine for “encroaching on someone else’s work.” For more on the rules, see [[belaya-zemlya|White, Grey, and Black Land]] and [[teni|The Shadow Esfera]].

## Carlos’s fort (ch. 7)
[[karlos|Carlos el Niña’s]] home is a former military bastion with cannon in the Old Port’s fort. Guards meet the “scribblers” at the entrance; in the courtyard, smuggled goods are being sorted, with a taster using thimble-sized cups, girls handling silks, and practice duels taking place. This is where [[alvaro-da-silva|Alvaro]] and [[fernan-gerejra|Fernan]] come to find the first hero of their planned book.

## The attack on Alvaro (ch. 18–20)
A statue of [[aj-khinar|Ai-Khinar]] stands at the entrance to the Old Port. The district is a haven for thieves, bandits, and prostitutes, and it is here, on his way back from Carlos, that Alvaro is struck with brass knuckles before his attacker shoots through his right palm with the words “Don’t write.” In chapter 20, he escapes the port by threatening a cabman and pimp with a revolver; in the Craft district, where the grey posts begin, the man throws him out of the two-wheeled carriage. It is later revealed that the gunman was Salvador ([[salvador|Salvador]]) (ch. 34).
