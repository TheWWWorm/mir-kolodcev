The Three Bulls is a tavern in [[laerta|Laerta]], standing on grey land where the Craft, Service, and Agriculture districts meet. Three emblems appear on its walls: a slipper for Service, a cogwheel for Craft, and a bull’s head for Agriculture. The name also belongs to an informal alliance of esferas, the left wing of the [[vysokij-stol|High Table]].

## A welcoming house (ch. 5, 11)
In chapter 5, [[alvaro-da-silva|Alvaro]], [[fernan-gerejra|Fernan]], and [[lyusil|Lucille]] dine here. Regulars discuss justice and how to reshape the High Table, pronounce the word “obrero” with pride, and refer to dons and maestros as “them.” One speaker complains that moving from one esfera to another is almost impossible. Chapter 11 describes the Three Bulls as an alliance of Craft and Service opposing the factory owners.

## Refuge and headquarters (ch. 20, 28, 30)
After escaping the [[staryj-port|Old Port]], the wounded Alvaro finds friends here: the tavern keeper recognizes him, and a doctor stitches his palm (ch. 20). Here, too, [[mor|Mor]] sets out the rebels’ “true aims.” In chapter 28, the characters discuss how the Three Bulls, hated by the “parasites,” will have to pay for mobilization; in chapter 30, the eating house has become an improvised strike headquarters, with a map of factories that have joined the strike and an infirmary upstairs. See [[reformisty|Reformists]] and [[stachka-v-laerte|Obrero Unrest in Laerta]].
