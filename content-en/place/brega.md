Brega is the industrial center of [[kataliya|Catalia]]. The author’s map labels it “Province of Brega—the industrial center of Catalia.” It has the largest coal deposits, and the broad River Monho serves as a transport artery. The novel says that Brega has more smokestacks than a forest has trees, and that when it rains, the drops turn black (ch. 20).

## A traveler’s view (ch. 4)
A traveler taking the train from [[laerta|Laerta]] spends another full day in Brega after [[seleziya|Selezia]]. Following the River Monho, sometimes approaching it and sometimes drawing away, the train passes towns packed close together, each bristling with rows of factory chimneys. Workshop sirens answer locomotive whistles, steamships and barges scurry along the river, and the carriage windows become coated in soot. Beyond Brega begins the Kabarda steppe.

## Industry and Esperanza (ch. 7–20)
Brega supplies the steam-powered machines that displace workers in Laerta (ch. 11), as well as compact engines; saltpeter for Brega’s cannon is imported from Muaris (ch. 17). Ten years earlier, Brega suffered the same unemployment crisis now beginning in Laerta. The engineer [[mor|Mor]], inventor of the pressure condenser, organized the dismissed workers, who challenged the dons to dueling debates and then, with their partners’ support, founded the [[esperantsa|Esperanza]] factory (ch. 20). Mor comes from Brega (ch. 34). Steam engines are manufactured at about ten factories around the country.

## History
The [[galereya-eliste|Eliste]] Gallery houses a battle panorama entitled “The Conquest of Brega.” The novel does not say exactly how or when Brega was conquered.
