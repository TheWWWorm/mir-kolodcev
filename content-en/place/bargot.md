Bargot is the capital of [[ollandiya|Ollandia]] (ch. 31). It stands in the south of the country, on the navigable River [[khursla|Khursla]], surrounded by wealthy principalities; this is the part of the country the Dangarian strategists covet most. On the author’s map, Bargot is labeled on the river’s southern bank, south of the red arrow marking [[kurtsleben|Kurtzleben’s]] army.

## The threat (ch. 31)
General of Infantry Kurtzleben, “a merciless butcher with an iron will,” leads the strongest of the three Dangarian armies southward. By chapter 31, he has yet to succeed: river supply arrangements have failed, and the Ollandians have fortified the southern bank and brought up mortars. Field Marshal [[rajniger|Reiniger]] gives him sixty days to cross the Khursla and besiege Bargot, but forbids a frontal assault: four of [[khofmajer|Hofmeyer’s]] divisions are to strike the flank as part of an outflanking maneuver. The novel does not reveal how the siege ends.
