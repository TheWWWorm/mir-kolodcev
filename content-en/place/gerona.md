Gerona is a city in Catalia.

## On the author’s map
It is labeled on the author’s world map in northern Catalia, beside the fortress of [[indra|Indra]] and south of the border with [[ollandiya|Ollandia]]. The name does not occur in the first book’s text, so neither its role nor its population is known. The nearest described stretches of the journey are Kabarda—a land known for its stubborn men, quarrelsome bulls, and bickering women—and the border fortress of Indra (ch. 4), through which the railway from [[laerta|Laerta]] to Ollandia passes.
