Mershants is an Ollandian fortress in the east of the country, near the Catalian border, and the center of the voivodeship known as Mershants. On the author’s map, it stands beside a large lake on the eastern frontier; an arrow for [[khofmajer|Hofmeyer’s]] army leads to it from [[latsen|Latsen]].

## The siege (ch. 14, 28, 31)
As early as chapter 14, we are told that Hofmeyer’s regiments are outside Mershants, that the campaign’s objective is to break through into Catalia and lay railway tracks, and that the merchants of Hofstadt are awaiting victory. In chapter 28, the Catalian general [[dezerej|Deserey]] reports that Hofmeyer now has twenty-five thirty-pounder siege mortars suitable for demolishing fortresses. Chapter 31 describes the fortress in greater detail: its walls are protected by two Catalian rifled howitzers with a range of about four versts, which mow down the Dangarian batteries; to the east lies a marsh that freezes in winter. Hofmeyer proposes leading two or three hundred dismounted “eagles” across the frozen marsh to the wall in winter, blowing it up, and disabling the howitzers; Field Marshal [[rajniger|Reiniger]] rejects the plan as suicidal. Major [[klyajn|Klein]] hopes the general will be delayed near Latsen, in which case he will have to take Mershants.
