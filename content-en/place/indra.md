Indra is a Catalian border fortress on the railway route to [[ollandiya|Ollandia]].

## In the text (ch. 4)
In chapter 4, where the reader travels by train from [[laerta|Laerta]], it is mentioned as the last stop: a town “compressed by a ring of fortifications like a maiden’s bosom by a corset.” An officer checks the passenger’s papers and wishes them a speedy return to Catalia, while Indra sees the train off with pennants waving from its bastions. Beyond Indra begins a belt of marshes, with the land of the Ollandians on the far side.

## On the author’s map
On the author’s map, Indra is labeled in the northern part of the blue region, north of [[brega|Brega]] and beside the city of Gerona. There are no other mentions in the text.
