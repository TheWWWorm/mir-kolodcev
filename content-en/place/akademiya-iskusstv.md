The Academy of Arts is the Art esfera’s institution of higher education in [[laerta|Laerta]]. Time here is marked by a tower: figures of the [[mirny|mirnas]] move along a circular balcony, and when the fertility mirna [[vinsenya|Vinsenya]] emerges beneath the enameled sun, it means that it is about two in the afternoon.

## Alvaro’s lectures (ch. 3, 13)
In the literature lecture hall, its walls hung with quotations from the classics, [[alvaro-da-silva|Alvaro]] teaches a series entitled “The Protagonist” (ch. 3). He adds poverty to his list of a hero’s attributes: a rich man inspires envy, a needy man is relatable to the reader, and a well-fed man wants nothing. The student in the front row with a golden fountain pen—[[beatris|Beatriz]]—argues with him, while a yellow-haired Reformist student ([[osa|Wasp]]) thanks him for his speech in the Chamber of the Word. Alvaro goes to the dean of the faculty of letters, [[farmiga|Don Farmiga]], to ask for an advance, but is refused: he drank away the previous advance and still owes five lectures. The dean threatens to replace him with the poet Cecilia Armani. In chapter 13, the series ends with a lecture on a hero’s flaws, which Alvaro delivers listlessly and without preparation.

## Other episodes (ch. 11–24)
In chapter 11, Alvaro gets rid of his persistent pupil Beatriz by writing to her father; in chapter 16, an important conversation takes place behind [[khose-flarbo|Jose Flarbo’s]] bookshop near the academy; in chapter 24, after a lecture, he leaves with Beatriz in her car. Nearby stands the Academy of Sciences, which once invited [[liess-van-khalm|van Halm]] to join it.
