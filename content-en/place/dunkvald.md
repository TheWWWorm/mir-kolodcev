Dunkwald is a settlement deep within [[dangar|Dangar]], concealed by thick forest. According to the account in chapter 27, it is inhabited by hundreds of stones belonging to the Fishermen’s Guild, all of whom do nothing but dig wells. They try different methods, conditions, and Zustands ([[tsushtand|Zustand]]), while apprentices record every attempt in ledgers. When water appears at the bottom, a [[fishmajster|fischmeister]] looks into it and gives his verdict, after which the well is filled in. In half a century, not one of these wells has become a [[kolodets|schönbrun]].

## The settlement (ch. 23, 27)
Dunkwald contains the women’s settlement of Katzen (its name is a joke), barracks, a temple of the Spiritual Sower, the administration’s mansion, dugouts, and a work clearing; the only road passable to vehicles leads to the town and the railway. When the settlement was founded half a century ago, it was taken seriously: the best masters came to test their theories, and the Kaiser sent agents. Now the unluckiest and lowest-born are posted here: Fischmeister [[joriksen|Fritjof]] and the Kaiser’s Claw [[klyajn|Wolf]] were assigned ten-year postings. Baron [[landau|Landau]] runs the settlement, with its guards and foremen under his authority.

## Red in Dunkwald (ch. 23–33)
In Red’s chapters, the settlement is not explicitly named until chapter 23. Here he digs “his own” schönbrun under a Zustand tailored to his nature (ch. 15, 19, 23); later, when the well proves ordinary, he is chained in a pit and made an executioner, a “beast” (ch. 33). In chapter 29, the magnus relates that a postman found Dunkwald full of dead people: about four hundred, including three platoons of soldiers, their bodies torn apart, some bearing bullet and knife wounds and others tooth and claw marks; the year’s records contain nothing suspicious. As early as chapter 14, society is already discussing the “terrible incident” in Dunkwald: the newspaper Glory reported that fishermen and officers had died.
