Solanta is a small island surrounded by dangerous reefs and currents in the Miriam Sea. It is linked to [[kataliya|Catalia]] by an alliance treaty signed, according to Count [[lurd|von Lurd]], thirteen years earlier (ch. 17); the exploit discussed in the novel took place sixteen years before the story begins (ch. 3). Its heroes were [[karlos|Carlos el Niña]], [[kristobal-maduro|Maduro]], [[dezerej|Deserey]], [[fatima|Fatima]], [[khuanes|Juanes]], and the deceased [[red|Rodrigo da Vira]].

## The island and the treaty (ch. 17)
The island had been a Muari base ([[muaris|Muari Emirates]]) against Catalian pirates of the Shadow esfera. Reefs protected it, but one pirate schooner passed through them at night, captured a coastal fort, and trained its cannon on the doge’s palace. The treaty was lenient: the doge allowed Catalia to purchase brante trees—buoyant raw material suitable for airships—and permitted its fleet to enter the ports under a flag of peace. As a result, the Muari lost their supremacy over the Miriam Sea.

## The expedition (ch. 22, 28, 34)
The story of what happened is revealed in pieces. Fatima is half-Solantan, her mother coming from the island, which is why she joined the expedition and conducted negotiations with the doge (ch. 28). Carlos bought a chart of the approach to the island from a Black sailor and, together with Maduro, planned the expedition to break the Muari monopoly over the channel. The navigator da Vira guided the ship through, and when the doge demanded the person who knew the passage, the captain handed him over (ch. 34).
