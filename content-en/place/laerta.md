Laerta is the capital of [[kataliya|Catalia]] and the principal setting of Traces on the Water. [[alvaro-da-silva|Alvaro da Silva’s]] story begins and ends here; the convict [[red|Red]] also longs to return to Laerta, his hometown.

## Location
Laerta stands on the southern coast, in the part of the country known as Beautiful Catalia: a land of warm weather, theaters, universities, and architectural gems. In the Dangarian generals’ assessment, the sea belongs to Laerta. On the author’s map, Laerta is labeled at the very south of the blue region, with a dotted route arrow leading away from it and marked “Beginning of the Solantan Expedition’s Voyage”; to the northwest, the map shows [[ortiyar|Ortiyar]] Fortress, guarding the approaches to the city.

## The city’s layout
The city is divided into eleven districts, one for each esfera. The districts have their own emblems: a slipper for Service, a cogwheel for Craft, and a bull’s head for Agriculture. Districts are colored according to the rules of the “lands”: in **white** areas, such as the Arts district, there are no locks or guards and murder is virtually impossible; **grey** areas, such as the Craft district and the meeting point of districts near the [[tri-byka|Three Bulls]] tavern, permit theft and fighting but not murder; **black** areas, by the sea in the [[staryj-port|Old Port]], allow almost any violence for those with “black” bracelets. For more, see [[belaya-zemlya|White, Grey, and Black Land]]. The High Table meets in the city center; the hall of the Great Scroll stands on the northern outskirts ([[svitok|Great Scroll]]).

## Familiar places
- **Del Toro Boulevard**, in the very center, lined with chestnut trees; marble [[mirny|mirnas]] stand on the roof of the [[galereya-eliste|Eliste Gallery]], with a queue outside the [[grand-opera|Grand Opera]] nearby.
- The **[[palata-slova|Chamber of the Word]]**, a building shaped like a bunch of grapes where debates are held, and the **[[akademiya-iskusstv|Academy of Arts]]**, where Alvaro lectures. There is also an Academy of Sciences and a university.
- The **Valentian Gardens and former royal palace** on West Avenue: Dr. [[khuanes|Juanes]] lived here. The Bridge Bastion once protected the northern approach to the palace, which now houses a museum and restaurants.
- **Craft Square**, with the temple of Darreggio ([[darredzho|Darreggio]]), and the general staff headquarters, with an avenue of twenty old cannon shaped like sea monsters.
- The **waterfront**: Alessina Bay ([[alessina|Alessina]]), with the dons’ yachts; Christopher’s Port, with grey posts; and the [[staryj-port|Old Port]], with a black one.
- The Church of Vinsenya ([[vinsenya|Vinsenya]]), the North Garden, arched bridges, and the teaching hospital named after Infanta Teresa of Mergana.

## The city in the story (ch. 0–34)
The novel opens with Alvaro’s evening stroll along Del Toro Boulevard with his friends, [[fernan-gerejra|Fernan]] and [[lyusil|Lucille]] Gereira: spring sunshine, newspaper boys shouting that steam-powered machines are displacing obreros, and Grand Opera posters advertising a premiere with Don Filippe. Then Laerta hosts the [[duelnyj-spor|dueling debate]] with Ramires (ch. 1); Don Filippe commissions a book about the heroes of Solanta (ch. 3); and Dr. Juanes dies (ch. 5). The Old Port becomes the scene of an attack on Alvaro (ch. 18), and the Three Bulls the Reformists’ strike headquarters (ch. 20, 30). In chapter 28, Laerta learns from General Deserey that the Dangarian troops are not leaving Ollandia, and a debate over mobilization begins in the city. Alvaro’s storyline ends with his return to Laerta from Tres Molinos together with [[fatima|Fatima]] (ch. 34).
