Latsen is a city in the center of [[ollandiya|Ollandia]], on the Plyutva–Latsen–Mershants railway line. On the author’s map, three red arrows fan out from it: [[kurtsleben|Kurtzleben’s]] army heads south, [[brojenshtoff|Brojenstoff’s]] north, and [[khofmajer|Hofmeyer’s]] southeast. The broadest arrow points toward Latsen from the western border, marking the invasion’s general direction, while a nearby black arrow is labeled “This is where Dietrich won the battle.”

## Occupation (ch. 6)
The Dangarians captured the city roughly three months before chapter 6. The square is clean and a brass band plays, but there are almost no listeners, and only women wait for water at the fountain. Here [[ditrikh|Dietrich]] meets [[olkha|Olkha]], while Major [[klyajn|Klein]] of the Kaiser’s Claws receives him in the town hall.

## Headquarters (ch. 25, 28, 31)
In chapter 31, Latsen is described as the country’s geographical center, whose capture split it into north and south. Field Marshal [[rajniger|Reiniger]] has installed himself in the former assembly hall of the Latsen Voivodeship’s nobility; the generals’ conference also takes place here. In chapter 28, the Catalian general [[dezerej|Deserey]] reports that the Plyutva–Latsen–Mershants railway is under construction, and that Kurtzleben’s and [[brojenshtoff|Brojenstoff’s]] armies are in the country’s center. According to chapter 25, Dietrich serves in Latsen, far from the fighting; in chapter 31, gendarmes arrest him at the station on Klein’s instructions.
