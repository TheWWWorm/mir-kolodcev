**Ugo Martinez** is a mustachioed chauffeur who becomes an accidental victim of Alvaro and Fernan's suspicions.

## A Night at the Inn (ch. 11)
While returning from [[ortiyar|Ortiyar]], Fernan convinces Alvaro that a green steam carriage is following them. At night, the friends go down to the inn's lobby, and Alvaro grabs the mustachioed chauffeur by the collar. The man introduces himself as Ugo Martinez and has no idea what is happening. Two children run into the lobby, shower the friends with rice, and shout “Happiness and freedom!”: Martinez divorced his wife today and is celebrating with his children and his sister, Aunt Nelly. He explains that they have been enjoying themselves in Laerta and are now returning, that they have not been to Ortiyar, and that the green bracelet on his wrist confirms his profession as a chauffeur. The embarrassed friends apologize under a hail of rice “canister.”

## Consequences
Alvaro is angry with himself for yielding to Fernan's suspicions, but immediately sees a plot device in what happened: [[beatris|Beatriz]] did not witness his humiliation. In the morning, he spots a green Cherrato 3-5 in the inn's courtyard and concludes that he has found the spy. He deliberately proposes to Beatriz that they question him together, knowing that out of stubbornness she will forbid him to take the risk—and Beatriz agrees to leave for [[laerta|Laerta]] immediately. In ch. 20, Alvaro admits to Beatriz that Martinez was not a spy, although a real spy did exist after all.
