The young scribe is an unnamed scribe's assistant at the camp. Red calls him by the same name as the author does.

## Scribe (ch. 4–19)
In ch. 4, he is a pimply, fair-haired lad of about seventeen. He comes out to announce the slaves' Zustands, cannot believe Red's entry, runs to check it, and returns with “kein Zustand.” He is afraid of Red, but measures the pit: Red himself has not been measured in it, so it cannot be a grave. In ch. 12, he inspects the completed well and records it in the ledger. In ch. 19, he enters the observations of the clearing in the book.

## The Supply Convoy (ch. 27)
In ch. 27, Red attacks a supply convoy, and the young scribe is among those left alive and addresses Red as “Herr Stein.” That night, Red asks whom he prays to, the Sower or Red himself. The boy prays to Red, who releases him with orders to carry the “true faith” to the people. [[yakka|Yakka]] asks him to spare the boy.

## A Future Fischmeister (ch. 33)
Years later, Red scarcely recognizes the grown young man, who has lost his pimples and become “a solid, well-fed hare.” The young scribe comes to the pit with [[greta|Greta]] and announces that he will receive a fischmeister's cloak and buy her. In his speech, Greta is almost a saint, while Red is an animal. Later, Greta turns out to be pregnant. The novel does not say whose child it is.
