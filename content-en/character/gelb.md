Gelb is a noncommissioned officer commanding a fusilier platoon composed almost entirely of stones. He is coarse and faultfinding: during [[ditrikh|Dietrich’s]] inspection, he shouts at a soldier for a stain on his uniform and promises ten lashes, until the lieutenant cancels the punishment. He knows his men: he calls the holy-fool stone Salvation and considers Killnot, who cannot kill, the platoon’s “most valuable soldier.”

## The night of the escape (ch. 2–6)

In the morning, Dietrich finds Gelb in a hut: he has been nailed to the wall through his palms and feet, and his jaw is smashed. He is badly wounded but alive. Dietrich orders [[spasenie|Salvation]] to remove the nails and bandage his wounds, but runs off himself at the sound of horses. By the end of the battle, Gelb is dead. Dietrich gives Major [[klyajn|Klein]] a different account, claiming that he tried to carry the wounded man, while Klein suggests that Gelb was abandoned to die.

## Escape (ch. 33)

In ch. 33, the rifleman Killnot from his platoon is revealed to be [[red|Red]]. Gelb himself does not appear in this chapter.
