Golden Ducat is the quartermaster at [[karlos|Carlos el Niña’s]] fort, a man with a gold tooth who addresses people as “you devil.”

## Meeting in a tavern (ch. 13)
Alvaro, having written a despairing letter to [[filippe|Filippe]] rejecting the commission, meets Ducat in a tavern. Ducat passes on the captain’s message: he is waiting for the book and will kill Alvaro if he dies before finishing it.

## At the fort (ch. 18)
When Alvaro comes to inquire about [[khuanes|Juanes’s]] death, the sentry calls Ducat. He takes the writer to the tower and whispers, “Try, do something, go easy on him.” Later, he joins the drinking in the study. Ducat remembers about forty foreigners — Muari, Merganans, and islanders — who sailed under el Niña’s flag, and insists that none of them ever shed blood beyond a white post: a foreigner would be the first suspect, and the punishment for a white-land murder is terrible, which he illustrates with gestures. “There are no white-land murderers,” he says. He knows nothing about da Vira, although he has served for years.
