**Don Farmiga** is dean of the Faculty of Letters at the Academy of Arts, where [[alvaro-da-silva|Alvaro]] lectures on literature.

## Refusing an advance (ch. 3)
After a lecture, Alvaro tries to obtain an advance from the dean for several future classes. Farmiga reminds him that the last time the lecturer received money, he disappeared for two weeks and owed the academy five classes. He threatens that if Alvaro continues disappearing, his lectures will be given instead by Cecilia Armani, Olesia’s finest female poet, who has moved to Laerta.

## Demanding an outline (ch. 13)
In ch. 13, Farmiga writes Alvaro a letter demanding that he urgently send an outline of his forthcoming lectures. Fernan puts the letter on the desk with the rest of the unread mail.
