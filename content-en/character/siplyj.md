Hoarse is the nickname Red gives one of the four slaves in his first dugout: by evening, his voice gives out and he mutters prayers. His real name is not stated in the novel.

## The Dugout (ch. 8–12)
In ch. 8, when Red breaks the work gang's resistance, Hoarse drops to his knees of his own accord, crying, “Nein! Bitte, nein!” He then sits down to eat at the common table. In ch. 12, he is the one who explains to Red through gestures that the foreman withheld food not arbitrarily but according to the law: only those who work are fed, and he should not be angry about the lashes.

## After the Escape (ch. 15)
When Red lives separately, Hoarse and [[boroda-lopatoj|Spadebeard]] encounter him at the spring and “goggle at him as if he were a winged toad.” Later, he comes with [[brigadir|the foreman]] to Red's dugout to look for a noose mark and leaves empty-handed.
