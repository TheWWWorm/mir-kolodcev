Schultz is one of the [[brigadir|Foreman’s]] four subordinates in the dugout where [[red|Red]] is placed in ch. 4. Red can barely distinguish Dangarian names and remembers only the red-haired fellow with the split eyebrow.

## Ch. 4–8
Schultz is the first to dare object when the Foreman orders Red beaten. He receives the worst bunk, beside the entrance. In ch. 8, red-haired Schultz, his feet frostbitten, passes Red his supper and huddles beside the stove. After the morning beating, Red knocks him unconscious against the stove.

## Frostbite (ch. 12)
When Red’s well reaches its seventh yard, Schultz suffers frostbite in his right foot, and his toes are amputated. He struggles with fever for three days and receives no food: by law, only those who work are fed. Red fills a bowl himself, but Schultz refuses out of fear, pulls a felt boot over the bloodied dressing, and returns to his Zustand on the fourth day.

## The digger (ch. 19)
In ch. 19, Baron [[landau|Landau]] shows Red the camp: Schultz digs his pit naked below the waist, with no toes on one foot and rotting toes on the other. Red doubts that he will live until spring, but Schultz continues digging.
