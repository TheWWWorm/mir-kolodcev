**Frey** is the woman who drives [[fatima|Fatima Ir-Reya’s]] black steam carriage and guards her employer. She barely speaks in the novel, but appears armed at the moments when she is needed.

## The stranger’s chauffeur (ch. 24–28)
In ch. 24, during his meeting with the stranger, Alvaro notices a gloomy female chauffeur with hair cut short “like a boy’s,” contemptuously smoking a pipe while he escapes the anatomical theater. She is named only in ch. 28: Alvaro calls her “the sullen creature who drives your car,” and Fatima corrects him: this is Frey, and “there have been recorded occasions when Frey smiled.” Fatima’s housekeeper and companion is named Yasmina.

## Last Winter (ch. 34)
In ch. 34, Frey enters the castle cellar first, moving with an experienced gunfighter’s gliding gait, instantly assesses the situation, and holds the wounded killer at gunpoint. A wisp of smoke rises from her revolver’s barrel; when [[beatris|Beatriz]] says they managed without help, she replies that they “certainly did.” Fatima explains that she came to [[tres-molinos|Tres Molinos]] incognito with only Frey: two bored ladies inspected the castle, took [[khavyer-salam|Javier Salam’s]] tour, and found a rusty component in the castle. Later, Frey remarks that Fatima and Alvaro’s kiss “stinks of burned meat,” matching the tone of Beatriz and Fernan.
