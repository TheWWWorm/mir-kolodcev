“Spadebeard” is the nickname Red gives one of the Foreman’s four subordinates. The novel does not give his real name.

## The dugout (ch. 8)
Every evening he returns from work with clumps torn out of his beard and smooths over the bare patches. In ch. 8, when Red breaks up the gang, Spadebeard receives a blow to the Adam’s apple.

## Ch. 15–19
In ch. 15, he meets Red at the spring with [[siplyj|Hoarse]] and stares at him, unable to believe he is alive. In ch. 19, Baron [[landau|Landau]] shows Red his pit: Spadebeard does not have to dig with his bare hands; instead, he plucks a hair from his beard and puts it in each shovelful of earth. Red even feels relieved: one could live with a Zustand like that.
