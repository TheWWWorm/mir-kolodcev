Flammel is an oberfischmeister of the Fishermen’s Guild. He does not appear in person in the novel: Magnus [[amir-sedoj|Amir the Grey]] and [[joriksen|Fritjof Joriksen]] speak about him.

## Investigation (ch. 29)
The Grey assigned Flammel to investigate the destruction of [[dunkvald|Dunkwald]]. He recently interrogated Joriksen and prepared a report that the magnus read “with a smile on his lips”: it reveals that the master is hiding something. The Grey knows Joriksen lied to Flammel and says the master apparently has his own grievances against the oberfischmeisters: despite his talents, Joriksen is still not one of them.

## Joriksen's Assessment (ch. 31)
Describing the interrogations to Major [[klyajn|Klein]], Joriksen says Flammel does not fully understand his own power, which made him easy to deal with. The Grey proved truly dangerous. The novel does not reveal how Flammel's investigation ended.
