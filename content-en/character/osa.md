**Wasp** is a Reformist student at the Academy of Arts, one of the few supporters of reform in that esfera. His name is a pseudonym: members of the secret society call one another by nicknames, and it is better not to know the real surname in the register.

## Introduction (ch. 3)
In ch. 3, Wasp approaches [[alvaro-da-silva|Alvaro]] after a lecture, thanks him on behalf of “all of us” for defending equality in the [[palata-slova|Chamber of the Word]], and explains that [[fernan-gerejra|Fernan]] and [[lyusil|Lucille]] told the Reformists about the debate. He declares that their society has almost no members in the esfera of Art: only himself and Alvaro.

## The riot and the invitation (ch. 11–13)
In ch. 11, Wasp is among the rioters at the station and attaches himself to his mentor. In ch. 13, he delivers an invitation from [[mor|Mor]] to the [[tri-byka|Three Bulls]]: the Reformists have a plan and are counting on Alvaro. Wasp looks at the maestro with adoration and considers him the ideal hero.

## Suspicions and the letter (ch. 22–24)
In ch. 22, [[beatris|Beatriz]], considering who might be behind the surveillance, names Wasp: she does not trust him. In ch. 24, he springs in front of Alvaro and Beatriz with a message from Mor, and when Alvaro refuses to listen, hands him a letter. Alvaro is about to tear it up, but Beatriz persuades him to take it with him.
