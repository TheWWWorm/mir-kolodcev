Kurtzleben is a Dangarian general of infantry, one of the three commanders of the armies that entered [[ollandiya|Ollandia]]. He is seen in person only in ch. 31; before that, he is named among those whose regiments are moving east and among Dietrich's possible powerful patrons.

## Mentions (ch. 2–25)

In ch. 2, he is mentioned together with [[khofmajer|Hofmeyer]]: their regiments advanced east, following the Ollandian infantry defeated near [[plyutva|Plyutva]]. In ch. 14, Fischmeister [[joriksen|Joriksen]] asks [[ditrikh|Dietrich]] whether “Field Marshal Kurtzleben” sent him—even though in ch. 31 Kurtzleben is called a general of infantry, while Reiniger is the one called field marshal. In ch. 25, as he says goodbye to his wife, Dietrich says Kurtzleben is advancing decisively and may take the capital by winter.

## The Conference in Latsen (ch. 31)

The narrator calls Kurtzleben a ruthless butcher with an iron will, commanding the strongest of the three armies. He has been ordered to advance south, where the wealthy principalities, access to the sea, and the largest navigable river, the [[khursla|Khursla]], lie, and to capture the country's capital, [[bargot|Bargot]]. His army was to be supplied by steamships traveling by sea and up the Khursla, but the ships did not arrive: [[mergana|Mergana]], through whose territory part of the route passed, lodged a protest and refused passage. Negotiations settled the matter, but the ships had already departed on other routes, leaving the cargo in port: half the provisions rotted and the gunpowder grew damp.

At the conference with Field Marshal [[rajniger|Reiniger]], Kurtzleben asks permission to force a crossing of the Khursla, promising to fill the river with stones and cross over. The field marshal replies that little of the army would remain afterward. Kurtzleben declares that he serves the emperor and recognizes no such thing as unacceptable losses. In the end, he is given sixty days to make a flanking maneuver, with four divisions from Hofmeyer's army, reach Bargot, and lay siege to it. Kurtzleben meets Hofmeyer's idea of a night assault on Mershants across the ice with a snort and calls it nonsense.

## In Catalia (ch. 28–32)

In ch. 28, General [[dezerej|Deserey]] says that the armies of Kurtzleben and [[brojenshtoff|Brojenstoff]] remain in central Ollandia six months after the country's surrender. In Deserey's journals (ch. 32), Kurtzleben is called a butcher; according to the entry, he did indeed capture Ollandia's capital, and the journal contains a detailed plan of the siege.

## At the End of the Novel (ch. 32)

Kurtzleben does not appear in person after ch. 31. According to Deserey, his army remains in central Ollandia.
