Carmensita Estevos is an obrero of the esfera of Shadow, a woman from a harbor brothel, and mother of [[red|Rodrigo da Vira’s]] son. She does not appear in person in the novel. Information about her comes from two sources: [[alvaro-da-silva|Alvaro da Silva]] finds her in the records of the [[svitok|Great Scroll]], while Red, in Dangarian captivity, remembers the mother of his son — Kara.

## Kara and Carmensita
The novel does not explicitly state that they are the same woman, but everything fits. Red is da Vira, as revealed in ch. 34. Kara is an “obrero of Shadow, a girl in your trade”; Carmensita is an obrero of the esfera of Shadow from a harbor brothel. Both bore a son to the same man, and the son remained in Laerta. Red writes from Dangar to the boy’s mother, and in ch. 34 Salvador says that da Vira wrote to Carmensita from Dangar. “Kara” is a usual shortened form of “Carmensita.”

## Kara in Red’s story (ch. 23–27)
In ch. 23, [[greta|Greta]] asks Red whether he loves his son’s mother:
> “Kara is an obrero of Shadow, a girl in your trade. She has a kind heart. But I would be a fool to love her, and she would be a fool to love me.”

Red plans to return to Catalia, take his son from Kara, “settle everything with her,” and marry Greta. The letter he carries concerns his son: if the reply arrived in time, the boy should be alive. In ch. 27, before the inevitable punishment, Red “ached not for himself, but for his son and Kara, and now for Greta too”; he loved Greta “far more than Kara… but less than his son.”

## The entry in the Scroll (ch. 22)
A few months after the [[solantijskaya-ekspeditsiya|Solantan Expedition]], she petitioned the court to recognize her as [[red|Rodrigo da Vira’s]] widow and her infant as his son. Her claim was denied for lack of evidence, and she disappeared. Alvaro concludes that [[fatima|Fatima’s]] husband forced her to give birth in secret and used Carmensita to establish in the records the “truth” that Fatima had no son. This theory proves false.

## A thread leading to da Vira (ch. 34)
Captured in Last Winter castle, Alvaro names Carmensita to Salvador as a “slender lead”: she bore da Vira’s child, and he had simply frequented a sailors’ brothel. Salvador says that da Vira wrote to her from Dangar and asks whether she knows where he is now. Alvaro replies that she should not: either she is dear to da Vira and he told her nothing for her safety, or she is not dear to him.

## The child’s father (ch. 34)
The child mentioned in da Vira’s letter is the one who remained in Laerta; in [[red|Red’s]] storyline, this is the son he has never seen.
