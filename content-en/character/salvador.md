Salvador is a mercenary in the guard of the manufacturer [[kristobal-maduro|Maduro]]. For much of the novel, he remains a nameless threat; his name is revealed during the interrogation at the mansion.

## Interrogation at Maduro’s (ch. 11–13)
In ch. 11, when the train carrying machines for Don [[balboa|Balboa]] is stopped, Alvaro strikes the hired thugs with the butt of his revolver during the fight; the account in ch. 11 says that one is hit in the nose. In ch. 13, a man with a broken nose sits at a table in the guardroom of Maduro’s mansion: his name is Salvador, and he says that he fought on Balboa’s side. He interrogates Alvaro, accuses the Reformists of sabotage, and demands to know their aims. Not knowing them, the writer sets out his own program and leaves with his hand on his revolver: detaining someone on white land is a crime.

## The attack and threats (ch. 18–34)
The attacker in the Old Port — tall, with a hoarse voice — shoots through Alvaro’s palm in ch. 18 after ordering him not to write. His name is not given then; only in ch. 34 does Alvaro recognize the voice in the courtyard of Last Winter castle and address Salvador by name, reminding him that he ordered him to abandon the book and spared him in the Old Port. According to Alvaro’s own theory, Salvador found a severed finger in a workshop and sent it to Alvaro, without maiming anyone.

## The night at Last Winter (ch. 34)
Salvador and a gunman with a rifle kill one of [[dezerej|Deserey’s]] two agents, seriously wound the other, shoot Fernan in the leg, take [[beatris|Beatriz]] hostage, and interrogate Alvaro about [[red|Rodrigo da Vira]]. Salvador strikes Beatriz and Alvaro. Alvaro’s deductions suggest that Salvador’s hidden employer wants to find da Vira; Alvaro explains that the bolt-action rifle, the ease of killing on gray land, and the word “schönbrun” betray a Dangarian spy, a Kaiser’s Claw ([[kogti-kajzera]]). Salvador does not deny it.

He takes the prisoners to the boarded-up door of the south wing, where the Well is located, and pulls the nails out with a crowbar. Dozens of mirrors cover the cellar ceiling. Fernan puts out the lantern, then raises it; Salvador tilts his head back toward the mirrors, and Fernan strikes his face with the burning lantern. Alvaro shoots him; Salvador burns like a living torch, and the writer finishes him with a mercy shot. His surviving accomplice will later be arrested (see [[vtoroj-ubijtsa]]).

## Whom he was seeking (ch. 34)
Salvador is hunting [[red|da Vira]], the man whom the reader recognizes as [[red|Red]] from the Dangarian chapters. Salvador does not meet Red in person in the novel.
