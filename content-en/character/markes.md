Senora Marques owns a tiny coffee shop in [[tres-molinos|Tres Molinos]]. Nominally, it is a café with two tables and a gas stove, but in essence it is a “museum of misfortunes” assembled in memory of the disasters suffered by the owners of the House Above. The novel also has another Marques, a patient at the [[klinika-miloserdiya|Mercy Clinic]] (see below), who has no connection to her.

## A Tour of the House Above (ch. 32)
The locals choose [[khavyer-salam|Javier Salam]] as guide, with Senora Marques as the next most knowledgeable expert. She rides with them in the carriage and adds comments, then leads the visitors herself along the red ribbons. She and Javier put the ribbons up when they began giving tours: following them is safe, but stepping beyond them is forbidden.

She then tells the house's story in a frightening, low voice: “This house is cursed!” Its last noble owner, the hidalgo Ernesto Ermidoro, died in the lists after aiming his lance at his opponent's horse rather than the man; his heirs sold the castle to a condottiere, who was found dead beside his dead wife shortly after their wedding—a crossbow bolt and a noose; a bank robber later moved in and sealed himself inside his vault in an explosion he caused. The last owner before van Halm was Nivendey, who won a slave woman at dice, discovered she was the fifth wife of a Muari sultan, and ended up in a dungeon. This happened about fifteen years earlier.

The café displays objects illustrating these fates: a knight in armor with a broken neck, a skull pierced by a bolt, a noose, a deck with a torn seven of spades, a blinded chemist in a newspaper clipping, and a love letter with a misplaced comma. Marques says that after the last incident, the town council resolved to warn anyone wishing to live in the castle. She opened the café and told the philosopher [[liess-van-khalm|van Halm]] everything, to which he replied: “A curse is wonderful! My chance to look beyond the edge of the world.”

Alvaro asks whether she believes in the curse; she replies, “Is that a question of belief?” In her view, the curse does not act immediately, and it drove van Halm mad.

## Later (ch. 34)
[[fatima|Fatima]] says she visited Tres Molinos incognito with Frey and stopped at Marques's café while researching the history of the curses.

## The Other Marques (ch. 26)
At the [[klinika-miloserdiya|Mercy Clinic]], the orderly Miguel mentions a patient named Marques who believes he is a rooster and takes part in cockfights. He has no connection to the café owner.
