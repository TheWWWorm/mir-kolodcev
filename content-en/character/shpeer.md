Speer is a lieutenant commanding the battery’s first platoon. When [[zejdvist|Zeidvist]] dies, Dietrich takes command and Speer silently obeys (ch. 2).

## Role in the battle (ch. 2–6)

Formally, Speer should have replaced the commander. In ch. 6, [[ditrikh|Dietrich]] explains to Major [[klyajn|Klein]] that he acted as he did because Speer comes from the hares while he is an eagle, and the soldiers would be more likely to trust an eagle. Dietrich adds that Speer afterward admitted he had been right. After the battle, Speer and the gun-crew commanders attend to the wounded and prisoners. He is not mentioned again in the novel.
