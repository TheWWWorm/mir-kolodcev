The Second Killer accompanies [[salvador|Salvador]] in the events of ch. 34; for the night in the castle, see [[posledniaya-zima-shturm|“The Night at Last Winter”]].

## The night in the castle (ch. 34)
He fires from the top of the watchtower with a bolt-action rifle, a military invention that, according to Alvaro, cannot be bought in a shop. He kills one of [[dezerej|Deserey’s]] agents, seriously wounds another, and shoots Fernan in the leg. In the castle hall, he stands by while Salvador interrogates the prisoners. In the cellar, as Salvador burns, the second killer throws a grenade down the stairs; the blast stuns everyone. Alvaro fires at his silhouette and wounds him in the arm and leg.

When [[fatima|Fatima]] and Frey enter the cellar, the killer is tied up and taken away with the corpses. The novel does not say what happens to him afterward.
