Jose Flarbo owns a small basement bookshop near the [[akademiya-iskusstv|Academy of Arts]]. Alvaro visits in the hope of finding [[filosofiya-uzhasa|The Philosophy of Horror]].

## The shop and its rules (ch. 16)
Flarbo calls himself an “old joker.” He sells books only to those who know what they are looking for, and calls people who reveal the plot in advance “executioners of pleasure.” He informs Alvaro that he ranks only third in sales: ahead of him are the memoirs of Colonel el Cameda, who died in a duel, and van Halm’s book. The publisher has ordered Cameda displayed at eye level, while da Silva’s books should be placed where people “stumble upon them.” The shop has a rule: only those who have bought a book may flirt.

## The story of van Halm
Flarbo explains that [[liess-van-khalm|van Halm]], a native of Mergana, was invited by the Academy of Sciences twenty years earlier, lived on an estate in [[seleziya|Selezia]], and wrote about horror as a step beyond the edge of the ordered world. Then he dismissed the servants and shut himself away. When the door was broken down, the philosopher was found emaciated, having eaten curtains, book spines, and shoe soles.

In the shop, Alvaro meets a customer asking for his first novel — she is [[fatima|Fatima]], as revealed in ch. 28 — and puts the purchase on his account. Later, in ch. 18, Flarbo’s shop is mentioned as the place where Alvaro found inspiration.
