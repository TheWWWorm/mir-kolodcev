**Malena** owns a book café in [[laerta|Laerta]] known for good pastries and intelligent views on literature. She has known [[alvaro-da-silva|Alvaro]] for a long time, and in the novel their relationship mixes love, exasperation, and habitual care. Malena appears in person only once, in ch. 13; in the other chapters, Alvaro only thinks of or remembers her.

## Her Past with Alvaro (ch. 9)
In ch. 9, while arguing with Fernan about an affair with a student, Alvaro recalls why he left Malena: she was too jealous. It is one of his arguments for why an affair with [[beatris|Beatriz]] would be a commonplace mistake.

## Poverty and Awkwardness (ch. 11–16)
In ch. 11, left penniless, he dreams of a bouquet of roses and “an hour of reconciliatory passion” at Malena's, but abandons the idea: she would welcome him without money, yet arriving as a pauper would mean humiliating himself. In ch. 16, he wants to stop by her café to undo the bad impression he left at their last meeting.

## An Evening at the Café (ch. 13)
During his creative crisis, after three days in seclusion, Alvaro goes to the café. Malena is glad to see him; they talk late into the night, drink wine, and she praises his latest book. He replies that he will write no more and tries to throw away his maestro's bracelet; Malena takes it from him and reminds him that this has happened to Alvaro about ten times already, and that morning will set everything right. He kisses her but draws back at the last moment, calling himself unworthy, and leaves. Recovering from her bewilderment, Malena calls him hysterical and throws the bracelet after him. The next morning, Alvaro writes her a farewell letter; later, Fernan opens a letter Malena has sent, but Alvaro refuses to read it.

## Influence on the Plot (ch. 18)
In ch. 18, the narrator lists the chain of causes that returned Alvaro to work: first Malena tried to rouse him, then his friends, then Fernan reminded him about money, Prim-Allen Filippe gave him a dressing-down, and finally the mysterious stranger provided the last spark. Malena is the first of these “steps.”
