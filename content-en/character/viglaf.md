Wiglaf is Master [[joriksen|Fritjof Joriksen's]] assistant and appears only in Chapter 14. He is an enormous man with thick sideburns, wearing a sleeveless top, with a blue anchor tattoo on his right shoulder. He dresses and speaks like a sailor.

## Appearance
Wiglaf stands on the stage behind the [[kolodets|schönbrun]], his arms crossed over his chest. Master and assistant bow to the well in the same way.

## Assistant at the Fishing Party (ch. 14)
At the [[rybalka-valbrekht|fishing party at Baroness Walbrecht's]], Wiglaf moves through the rows of spectators, handing out slips of paper and pencils so everyone can write down the “heartache” they want to be rid of. He then collects the slips and presents them to Joriksen on a tray. Using the sea reckoning, Joriksen checks the date, time, and weather with Wiglaf, who answers as one should aboard a ship. The assistant is not mentioned again; Joriksen's secretary [[otto|Otto]] accompanies him on the journey to Ollandia.
