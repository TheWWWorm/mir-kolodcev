“The One Without Nails” is the nickname Red gives a companion in his first dugout. His real name is not stated in the novel.

## The Dugout (ch. 8–12)
In ch. 8, his nails are broken and his fingers scraped raw, as if he spent whole days clawing at a wall. When Red breaks the work gang's resistance, he twists the man's finger and punches him in the nose. In ch. 12, his thumb becomes infected.

## Zustand (ch. 19)
Baron [[landau|Landau]] shows Red the pit of The One Without Nails: he is on all fours, digging the earth with his bare hands, his fingers rotting. It is the first pit Red sees in the camp and one of the most horrifying.
