**Little One** is an exceptionally tall young obrero with a mechanical arm, one of [[mor|Mor's]] associates among the reformists. His name causes confusion in the novel: in ch. 11, the crowd shouts “Make way! Mor is coming!”, and Alvaro thinks he is seeing Mor himself.

## At the Station (ch. 11)
When the workers stand on the tracks facing the manufacturer's thugs, the giant pushes through the crowd, clearing a path with his iron claw and announcing Mor's arrival. No violence is needed: people make way of their own accord. When Fernan is knocked down, Alvaro grabs the giant's flesh-and-blood arm, and he lifts Fernan “like a cat” with a single steel claw. He then shouts, trying to stop the fight: “Mor is speaking!”

## An Introduction (ch. 20)
Only in ch. 20, in a room at Three Bulls, does it become clear that the giant is not Mor. Mor is the stocky older woman sitting beside Alvaro's bed, who says, “He is Little One. I am Mor.” In his deep voice, Little One supports her words: workers are being thrown into the street like rubbish, and the “bloodsuckers” must be ruined, he says, clenching his steel fist. Little One's role in the scene is to emphasize the rebels' “terrifying” aims, which Mor then refutes.
