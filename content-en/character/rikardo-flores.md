**Ricardo Flores** is [[alvaro-da-silva|Alvaro da Silva's]] grandfather on the Flores side of the family. In the novel, he is present only through his grandson's stories. Alvaro describes the Flores family as rich in poets, writers, and playwrights, and his grandfather as its only member to have created a literary masterpiece and inscribed his name on the [[svitok|Great Scroll]]. Alvaro himself is firmly determined to become the second.

## The Snake in the Reeds
Ricardo's major work is the novel The Snake in the Reeds, which his grandson calls a jewel of world literature. Alvaro calls his grandfather Don Ricardo and says he died basking in fame and wealth.

## Family (ch. 13)
In ch. 13, while explaining to the guard [[salvador|Salvador]] at Don [[kristobal-maduro|Maduro's]] how people become dons in Catalia—through skill, not inheritance—Alvaro talks about his family. His father, Ricardo's son, was overshadowed by his own father's brilliance: afraid of failing to live up to him, he spent his entire life as an obscure playwright at a small theater. He never dreamed of the title of don.
