Field Marshal Reiniger is the highest Dangarian commander in the Ollandian theater. In chapter 31, he holds a conference in the former assembly hall of the Latsen voivodeship’s nobility, now his headquarters, and announces changes to the campaign plan.

## The conference in Latsen (ch. 31)
Reiniger sits at the head of the table, gloomy as a storm cloud. Beside him are Lieutenant General [[khofmajer|Hofmeyer]] and Generals of Infantry [[kurtsleben|Kurtzleben]] and [[brojenshtoff|Brojenstoff]]. Reiniger listens to reports, but has already made his decision: the meeting’s purpose is to communicate new orders to his subordinates.

- Brojenstoff is to continue distracting the northerners in the mountains; he does not need to capture large territories.
- Kurtzleben is given sixty days to force a crossing of the [[khursla|Khursla]] and besiege [[bargot|Bargot]], but without a frontal assault: to avoid unacceptable casualties, four divisions from Hofmeyer’s army will make a flanking movement through the river’s upper reaches.
- Hofmeyer is left with half his army and is merely to maintain the siege of [[mershants|Mershants]]. When he proposes an assault across the frozen marsh by two or three hundred eagle cavalrymen, the field marshal rejects it as suicidal and orders him to dispatch the divisions within two days.

The field marshal values his subordinates differently: he sees Brojenstoff only as a diversionary force. He receives information about Catalian rifled howitzers from Hofmeyer and Major [[klyajn|Klein]]: two such howitzers are operating against Hofmeyer outside Mershants.

## Deserey’s assessment (ch. 32)
In the Catalian general [[dezerej|Gabriel Deserey’s]] diary, Reiniger is called “a vastly experienced coward.” Deserey analyzed the Ollandian campaign and assessed the enemy commanders from newspaper cuttings and reports.
